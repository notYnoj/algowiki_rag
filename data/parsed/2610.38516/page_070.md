The cross-entropy stays against the true instance map (`mapped\_cross\_entropy`): the target of a predicted label is the ground-truth nucleus holding the majority of its marker pixels (the owner rule of `marker\_quality`), so a marker in background has no pixel to win and its label is penalised wherever it wins; a nucleus with several markers keeps all of them, the probability of the target being their sum, so the loss does not force a split; the pixels of a nucleus without a marker can be right under no label and get zero weight (they stay in the denominator; `--mk-unmarked bg` makes the background label their target instead: the solve is then asked to hand an unmarked nucleus to the background rather than to a neighbour).
