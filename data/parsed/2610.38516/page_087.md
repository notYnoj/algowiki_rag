<tbc>_differentiable (candidate strengths trained through the solve) did not help; the gradient reaching the marker head degraded detection without improving the ranking of candidates._ Untried within the deadline, in decreasing order of promise: stopping the gradient into the marker head and training only \(\theta\), T (this reduces to a learned threshold on z, i.e. the fold-3 tuning, so a small gain at best); a separate strength channel trained only by the solve, so the distance head keeps its target; and a per-image normalisation of the strengths. One seed, one fold pair, one parametrisation (mean score, \(\theta_0=-1\), primary rule).
