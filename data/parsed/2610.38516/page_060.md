- **Where the remaining 0.17 F1 to the oracle is.** With the ground-truth markers the same costs give 0.959; the distance head leaves 14.5 % of the nuclei without a marker (22 % on the patches with more than 60 nuclei), and these are the whole of the gap in detections: DQ 0.791 against the oracle’s 0.959 is 0.17, and the fallback markers from the degenerate foreground mask do not recover them (-0.015 F1). The missed nuclei are those whose predicted distance map stays below 0.2 everywhere (which nuclei these are, small or faint, was not examined); the next step on markers is a detection objective (a focal or count-aware loss on the distance map, or the HoVer-Net horizontal/vertical maps whose gradient is sharp at every seam), not a lower threshold (thr 0.1 on the extended grid leaves 14 % without a marker and adds background markers: 4.7 per patch, PQ 0.628).<tbc>

- **The capped solve was not a problem, and the limit is still usable. maxit300** reproduces section 11’s costs (oracle identical) at 2.8 times the training cost; its marker head came out slightly noisier (one seed, one run). The exact K-label limit of the distance-head costs is 0.758 F1 / 0.603 PQ on the 307 patches, -0.008 F1 / -0.016 PQ / -0.014 IoU from the converged solve, the same small price as in section 11, so deploy-at-the-limit on one CPU core still gives essentially the WS layer’s instances.<tbc>

**- **What this says about the plan.** The multi-marker WS layer with a distance head for its markers is now the best nucleus instance segmenter in this report on F1 and PQ, by a margin (+0.076 F1 over the tuned watershed) that is larger than every other difference measured since section 6, with no post-processing beyond three scalars chosen on a held-out fold (the watershed has one), and the exact limit within 0.01 F1 of it. It remains one seed and one fold pair, and the contour-head U-Net was not given the distance head (a U-Net with a distance head + watershed on the distance map, the HoVer-Net recipe proper, is the fair next baseline: the oracle rows say the WS layer’s costs are not the limiting factor, so that baseline may well match the WS layer on F1, and the comparison would then be about the solver as the final assignment step against a watershed of the distance map).
