<tbc>inside the foreground mask anyway). Fold 3 chooses threshold \(\in\{0.2,0.3,0.4,0.5\}\times\text{nms}\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}\times\text{rfac}\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}\) by PQ on the usual 300 patches, for the WS layer (through its converged solve) and for the baseline (\(\times\) the three reliefs), never on fold 2. The training-time markers of the 11.5 schedule use threshold 0.3, nms 0.5, rfac 0.5. At most 256 peaks per patch enter the suppression (an untrained heatmap has thousands).

**The soft variant** (`--ctr-soft`, run `ctr\_bg\_soft`). Every marker enters the attachment with its peak value as a strength, a\_k = (\(\sigma\text{\_}\text{k}\) s)^p, i.e. c1 - log \(\sigma\text{\_}\text{k}\) on its pixels as in section 14, but \(\sigma\text{\_}\text{k}\) is a constant: the detection loss alone trains it, nothing flows from the solve into the selection (the failure mode of 14). The strengths are on at training time (on the predicted markers of the schedule) and at test time. In addition every centre run evaluates the other variant on the same markers and costs (`layer\_soft` for a hard-trained model, `layer\_hard` for a soft-trained one), so the test-time effect of the strengths is separated from the training effect.

**Checks before the runs** (`center\_check.py`, 16 evenly spaced patches of fold 1; `center\_check.txt`). The targets: one centre pixel per instance (565 for 565), all inside their instance, 565 heatmap peaks for 565 instances. The rule on the target heatmap and radius (an ideal prediction): every nucleus gets exactly one marker and no marker lands in background at rfac 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 (marker pixels inside their own nucleus 100 / 99.9 / 98.3 %), and the same on a blurred target with the radius spread over the Gaussian's support, with or without the suppression. A baseline trained for six epochs on the full fold gave, at threshold 0.3, 29 markers per patch for 35 nuclei (74 % of the nuclei with exactly one, 25 % none, 2.3 in background; threshold 0.2: 39 markers, 85 / 11 %, 5.8 in background), the radius within 1.06 px of the target on average, and the suppression changing the count by at most 0.4 markers per patch: the head produces about one peak per nucleus, not the hundreds of section 11.3, and the threshold trades recall against background markers smoothly.

**What was run** (seed 0, fold 1 \(\rightarrow\) fold 2, tuning on fold 3, all 2523 test patches). The WS layer with the section 11.5 recipe and the centre head in place of the distance head (`ctr\_bg`: `--mk-head center --w-mk 1 --mk-sched 10 15 --mk-unmarked bg`, p 5 \(\rightarrow\) 20, edge target; the predicted markers of the schedule at threshold 0.3 / nms 0.5 / rfac 0.5), its soft-strength variant (`ctr\_bg\_soft`), the fair baseline with the same head (`unet\_ctr`: the section 11.2 U-Net and watershed, the distance channel replaced by the heatmap and radius), and the three at 10 % of fold 1 (`ctr\_bg\_p10`, `ctr\_bg\_soft\_p10`, `unet\_ctr\_p10`: 266 images, 300 epochs, `--mk-sched 100 150`, as section 13). The references are `dist\_sched10\_bg` and `unet\_dist` (sections 11.5 / 11.2) and `dist\_bg\_p10` / `unet\_dist\_p10` (section 13). The WS layer runs took 3.7 h on an A100 (326 s per epoch, 36 settings \(\times\) 300 patches of fold-3 tuning through the solve in 31 min, the evaluation with its four converged solves per batch in 85 min); the baselines 11 s per epoch; the 10 % layer runs 1.3 h for the 300 epochs. Two layer jobs died at start on ruche's node gpu16 with "CUDA-capable device busy or unavailable" and were resubmitted elsewhere; the 10 % runs ran on dvaughan.
