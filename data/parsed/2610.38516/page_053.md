**The WS layer.** For K markers plus the background, label k gets an attachment a\_k \(\ge\) 0 per pixel and the K + 1 systems share the matrix:

`(L(w) + diag($\\Sigma$\_j a\_j)) x\_k = a\_k, k = 0 \\dots{} K, so that $\\Sigma$\_k x\_k = 1 (L 1 = 0).`

x\_k,i is the probability that a random forest at inverse temperature p connects pixel i to label k. The systems of every image of a batch are stacked along the batch axis (M = \(\Sigma\)\_b (K\_b + 1) systems, the weights gathered per image), so the PCG of section 1 runs unchanged on (M,1,H,W) tensors, K is free per image, and the cost is affine in K with no padding. The solve is implicit; the adjoint is one solve per label with the same matrix, dl/dw\_e = -\(\Sigma\)\_k (x\_k,u - x\_k,v)(\(\lambda\)\_k,u - \(\lambda\)\_k,v) and dl/da\_k,i = \(\lambda\)\_k,i - \(\Sigma\)\_j \(\lambda\)\_j,i x\_j,i (own right-hand side, shared diagonal), which reduces to the binary formulas of section 0 for K = 1. Attachments: a\_0,i = exp(-p c0\_i) everywhere, c0 = -log(1 - s) the U-Net’s background cost; a\_k,i = exp(-p c1\_i) on the marker pixels of label k with c1 = -log s and zero elsewhere, so the markers are soft (their strength is the network’s foreground confidence) and differentiable; the edge costs are the learned cost head of sections 6.1 and 10 (scale \(\cdot\sigma(\mathrm{h})\), learnable scale, edge target).

**The limit with K labels. power\_watershed** of section 1 interpolates scalar seed values on the plateaus it has to solve, which is the exact limit for two labels only. `power\_watershed\_multi` keeps a K-vector per merged node: seeds are one-hot, a plateau that touches two distinct known vectors is solved with K right-hand sides at once (unit-weight RW), and the argmax is taken at the end, so it is the limit of the K systems above and not an interpolation of label indices. Self-tests (\(7\times\) 9 grid, random costs, K = 3; \(12\times\) 14 grid with costs quantised to 1/4 so that large ambiguous plateaus occur): the label probabilities sum to one to 1e-14, the two-label case equals the binary WS layer and the binary limit, the adjoint matches finite differences to seven digits on every input, the argmax of the finite-p solve equals the limit’s at p = 60, and on the quantised grid the K-vector limit equals the K + 1 one-hot runs of the scalar `power\_watershed` exactly.

**Training** (fold \(1\rightarrow\) fold 2 as before, 30 epochs, batch 16, one seed). The U-Net has four output channels: the foreground logit (its sigmoid s gives c0 and c1), the two-channel cost head, and a marker logit. Training markers are the ground-truth instances eroded by 2 or 3 px (radius drawn per step; the core of a nucleus the erosion would empty is kept), the marker head is trained with BCE against them, and the WS layer loss is the per-pixel cross-entropy of the K + 1 label probabilities against the instance map (the seam pixels and their 1-px flank weighted \(\times\) 6), plus 0.5 BCE on the foreground logit and section 10’s edge target (\(\times\) 1) on the cost head. p is annealed \(5\rightarrow\) 20 (section 6.1: costs learned at low p do not transfer to p = \(\infty\), so both the finite-p output and the exact limit are reported).
