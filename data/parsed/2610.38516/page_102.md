**-** **Verdict.** A CenterNet-style head is a sound marker detector for this pipeline (one peak per nucleus, a radius within 0.7 px of the target, no needle failure, the threshold trading recall against background markers smoothly), and it is the first marker change since section 11.5 that does not cost the WS layer anything; but it does not improve it. The WS layer's result is pinned at F1 0.795 by a third marker head, which says that the ceiling is not the head's form but the detectability of the 12-20 % of nuclei that none of the heads, nor the baseline, finds at a threshold that keeps the background clean: the oracle gap of 0.07 F1 is shared by every variant. For the watershed baseline the distance head stays the right choice, because it doubles as the relief. The 10 % lead of the structured layer survives the change of head (+0.006 against the distance baseline, +0.031 against the matched one) but is largest with the distance head. One seed, one fold pair, one set of hyper-parameters (\(\sigma\) = 0.5 r\_eq, focal weight 1, radius weight 0.1) for the head; a lower marker-head weight or a second seed were not run.
