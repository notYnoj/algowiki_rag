## Is the exact limit the deployed object?

Not in these experiments. On the instance model, solved exactly at increasing _p_ with costs and markers fixed, accuracy peaks at the training _p_=20 and falls monotonically to the limit (0.795 to 0.724 _F_<sub>1</sub>), which the solve reaches by _p_≈500 (Appendix G). What a higher _p_ removes is the hedging: discounted markers become objects again. The model trained from true markers is the mirror image: within 0.006 _F_<sub>1</sub> of its limit, but worse on its own markers. A deployable limit and tolerance to imperfect markers pulled in opposite directions in every schedule tried. For the binary models the picture differs: pixel IoU is flat up to the training _p_ and then loses 0.05 at the limit, while instance _F_<sub>1</sub> keeps rising to _p_≈200, because a high _p_ removes the faint blobs that thresholding a soft output leaves ( 9 and the record section "Accuracy against _p_").

## Is there a training regime that lets the combinatorial watershed replace the WS layer at inference?

Partly, and the record says what it needs. Two trained models already have a deployable limit: the binary layer with the edge target trained at _p_ from 5 to 50 (limit 0.016 IoU below the converged solve, no loss on BF<sub>2</sub>; Sect. 4.3) and the multi-marker layer trained from ground-truth markers at _p_ from 5 to 20 (limit 0.006 _F_<sub>1</sub> below the solve on all of fold 2;  3 and the record section "The exact limit on all of fold 2"). What they share, and what the models with a lost limit lack: costs trained to cut between every pair of neighbouring markers with the background as a soft seed, which is what a maximum spanning forest needs (the binary models trained at low _p_ lost their limit entirely); sharp contours from the edge target, which cut the limit’s price from 0.046 to 0.016 IoU; and no hedging, since training from the model’s own markers or in the implicit mode makes the costs rely on finite-_p_ softness that the limit removes. Those last two are exactly our best finite-_p_ models, so the trade-off stated in the main text is the obstacle: the tolerance to imperfect markers lives in the softness, and the limit has none.
