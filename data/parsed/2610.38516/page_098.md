## W A detection head for the markers: CenterNet-style centres and radii

Sections 11.3, 11.4, 14 and 16 all tried to improve the markers through the distance map (a per-nucleus peak loss, hard negatives, the gradient term, strengths trained by the partition loss) and none helped; with ground-truth markers both the WS layer and the fair baseline reach F1 0.87 against 0.795 / 0.794 with their own, so marker detection is the whole remaining gap, and it is shared. one of us proposed a detection formulation instead: predict a centre and a radius per nucleus as a detector does (CenterNet / Mask R-CNN), aggregate the overlapping proposals, and use the disc as the marker. This section implements it for both models and measures it at full data and at 10 % of fold 1. : `--mk-head center` in `experiments/exp\_instance\_pannuke. py` (`center\_target`, `center\_focal\_loss`, `markers\_from\_center` with `center\_ peaks` / `disc\_iou`, `center\_strengths`; `marker\_loss` now returns the loss and its parts; the training-time extraction worker takes a dict; new outputs `layer\_soft` / `layer\_hard`) and `exp\_unet\_dist.py` (`DistSegmenter(center=True)`, `own\_ markers`), the checks `center\_check.py`, the cases `ctr\_bg`, `ctr\_bg\_soft`, `unet\_ ctr`, `ctr\_bg\_p10`, `ctr\_bg\_soft\_p10`, `unet\_ctr\_p10` of `ruche\_run.sh` and `run\_ center.sh` (jacobian / dvaughan), and the tables `compare\_center.py`. Results: `results/pannuke\_instances/{ctr\_bg, ctr\_bg\_soft, unet\_ctr}/`, `results/pannuke\_data\_efficiency/- ctr\_bg\_soft\_p10, unet\_ctr\_p10}/`, `compare\_18.md` and `compare\_18\_p10.md`, `center\_check.txt`; the `.pt` files stay on ruche / dvaughan.

**The head.** Two output channels replace the distance channel: the centre heatmap logit (channel 3, so that it is the marker map everywhere else in the code) and the radius (channel 4, softplus, in pixels). Targets from the instance masks, after the d4 augmentation: the centre pixel of every nucleus is its pixel nearest its centroid; the heatmap is the maximum over the nuclei of a Gaussian at the centre pixel with \(\sigma=\) clip(0.5 r\_eq, 1, 6) px, r\_eq = sqrt(area / \(\pi\)) the equivalent radius (mean 9.8 px on fold 1, 5 / 50 / 95 % 3.6 / 8.6 / 20.8), exactly 1 at the centres; the radius target is r\_eq at the centre pixels. Losses: the CornerNet / CenterNet focal loss on the heatmap (positives = the centre pixels, every other pixel a negative weighted (1 - target)^4, the (1 - p)^2 / p^2 modulation, normalised by the number of centres: explicit negatives everywhere, which is what the distance-map losses of 11.3-11.4 could not give without conflicting with the rim) and \(0.1\times\) the L1 of the radius at the centres; the marker-head weight is 1 (the focal loss is of order 1 after the first epoch, where the distance MSE was \(0.008\times 2\)). Foreground head, contour head and edge target unchanged.

**The marker rule.** Peaks = the pixels at the maximum of their \(3\times 3\) window and above a threshold, one per plateau; a greedy non-maximum suppression over the predicted discs (in decreasing peak order, a peak whose disc has IoU above `nms` with a kept disc is merged into it); marker k = the disc of radius `rfac` \(\times\) predicted radius (at least 1 px) around the peak, a pixel inside two discs going to the stronger, every marker keeping its peak pixel; optionally clipped to the foreground s _\>_0.5 (not used: the WS layer’s attachment c1 = -log s already makes a background disc pixel inert, and the watershed baseline’s markers lie<tbc>
