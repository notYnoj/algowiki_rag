If this thread continues, the one schedule-side item left is training-time thresholds that follow the tuning (the markers are extracted at the fixed 11.1 thresholds, while the tuning folds now prefer a minimum area of 1-9); the larger question, whether the solve’s softness can be traded back for the watershed’s boundary precision (a higher p at the end of training, or the exact limit as the inference step now that the costs are trained for imperfect markers), is open.<tbc>
