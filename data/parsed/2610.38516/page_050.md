## O.1 The fair baseline: a U-Net with its own contour head, and a watershed on it

**What was run.** The U-Net of section 6.1 (gap target, same recipe, fold 1 \(\rightarrow\) fold 2, 30 epochs, one seed) with three output channels like `rw\_learned` but no layer: channel 0 is the segmentation logit under BCE, channels 1-2 a per-pixel contour head trained with exactly section 10's edge target (BCE on the edge probability averaged onto each grid edge against the instance-id changes, positives \(\times\)10, seams \(\times\)5 on top, weight 1). It is evaluated as the thresholded logits and after two classical post-processings of the contour map c (mean of the two directions): (a) the segmentation with the predicted contour (c _\>_ 0.5) removed; (b) a marker-based watershed of c inside the segmentation, seeded by the maxima of the distance transform of the thresholded segmentation, with the watershed lines set to background (2 px, like the seams of the gap target), so that touching nuclei are split along the predicted contours. A second seeding, from the distance transform of (a) so that the contour does the first split, is reported as (b\('\)). The one hyper-parameter, the marker spacing, was chosen by PQ on 300 patches of fold 3 (neither fold used elsewhere): 12 px for (b), 25 px for (b\('\)); on fold 3 the PQ of (b) goes from 0.42 at 2 px to 0.59 at 12 and 0.57 at 25, so the choice matters and is not the WS layer's advantage.
