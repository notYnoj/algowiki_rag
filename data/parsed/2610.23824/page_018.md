## AI Usage Disclosure

The authors substantially used OpenAI’s large language model, GPT-6 Astra, during the proof exploration phase. Through an iterative process with the authors, the model generated the proof of the main theorem (Theorem 1) for the special case _k_=3. Then, the authors simplified the proof, discussed and obtained the intuition behind the algorithm, and generalized the analysis to all \(k\ge 3\).
