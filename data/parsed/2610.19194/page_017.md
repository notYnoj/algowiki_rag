## Tool use declaration

The enumeration and duality computations were performed with Python programs `one\_cycle\_chord\_diagrams.py` and `general\_codes.py` Prishlyak (2026a,b); the figures were typeset with LATEX and TikZ.

Generative AI (Claude, Anthropropic) was used as follows: (i) writing and debugging parts of the Python code, including the scripts that generate the catalog of chord diagrams in the Appendix (conversion from the computational code to the drawing code, canonical forms, dual and self-dual diagrams); (ii) generating the LATEX/TikZ source of the Appendix figures from the program output; (iii) drafting and editing the English text of the description of the algorithms and of some passages of the Introduction and Conclusion, and suggesting arXiv subject classes.

All mathematical statements, definitions and results come from the authors and from the cited literature. The authors checked the program output independently (for example, by verifying that taking the dual twice returns the original diagram and that the counts of diagrams are consistent with the known ones), reviewed and edited all AI-generated text and code, and take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
