Taking another logarithm and using \(log(a+b+c)\le log3+max\{loga,logb,logc\}\), we get<tbc>

\(loglogn\le c_1+max\{loglogH,log\eta\}\le c_2+\eta\)

<tbc>for universal constants _c_<sub>1</sub>_,c_<sub>2</sub>_\>_0, because \(log\eta\le\eta\). Since \(1+\lfloor log_2n\rfloor\le c_3logn\) for some universal constant _c_<sub>3</sub>_\>_0, we have

\(x_n(\gamma)=log\big(\frac{\pi^2\big(1+\lfloor log_2n\rfloor\big)^2}{3\gamma}\big)\)<br>
\(\le log\big(\frac{c_4(logn)^2}{\gamma}\big)\)<br>
\(=logc_4+log\big(\frac{1}{\gamma}\big)+2loglogn\)<br>
\(\le c_5\left[log\big(\frac{1}{\gamma}\big)+loglog(max\{H,e\})\right]=c_5\eta,\)

for universal constants _c_<sub>4</sub>_,c_<sub>5</sub>_\>_0. Choosing \(C\ge c_5\), we obtain<tbc>

\(n\ge CH\eta\ge Hx_n(\gamma).\)

<tbc>Consequently, condition (1) is satisfied. Since \(\alpha_L=1+log\frac{2\sigma}{\beta}\) , we have \(H=\Theta\left(\frac{\sigma^2}{\beta^2}log\frac{\sigma}{\beta}\right)\), and therefore

\(n=O\left(\frac{\sigma^2}{\beta^2}log\frac{\sigma}{\beta}\left[log\frac{1}{\gamma}+loglog\left(e+\frac{\sigma}{\beta}\right)\right]\right).\)

This completes the proof.
