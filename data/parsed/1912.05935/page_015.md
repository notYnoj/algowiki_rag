As with the _randSet & randSet_ model, the retest sample size in most cases was one million. For other values of _n_, the sample size gradually decreased from 10<sup>5</sup> to 100. Both algorithms are based on the principle of random selection. Therefore, it should be expected that the conclusions drawn here will be basically identical to the conclusions formulated for the _randSet & randSet_ model. a) the _rand & rand_ model does not work as hard as the _randSet & randSet_ model.
