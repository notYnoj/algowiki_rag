The preservation proof maps an asteroidal set of _A<sub>G</sub>_ to an asteroidal set of _G_ by taking the leaf coordinates: the same path argument applies to any two members while avoiding a third. Combined with the bounded-asteroidal-number result of Broersma et al. [6], this gives polynomial-time solvability for each fixed bound on asteroidal number. Whether the dependence can be made fixed-parameter tractable is a more precise further question.
