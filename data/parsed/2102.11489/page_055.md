_Proof._ Follows directly due to the algorithm of Theorem 5.5 where we implement the nearly orderly segmenter and the sorter for a nearly orderly array using the circuits of Facts 15.5 and 15.6, respectively. Further, we use Lemma 6.1 to convert each circuit gadget in our operational model to a constant fan-in, constant fan-out boolean circuit gadget.
