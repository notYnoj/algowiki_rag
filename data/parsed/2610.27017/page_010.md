The approximation properties of prolongation operators were studied in \[28\]. In particular, sufficient conditions were established under which a BTTB matrix generated by a nonnegative even function ensures that the \(I_H^h\) defined by (3.2) satisfies (3.8). More precisely, the following lemma holds.

**Lemma 3.3.** \[28\] If the nonnegative even function \(f(\theta_1,\theta_2)\in C^1[-\pi,\pi]^2\) satisfies:

\(min_{(\theta_1,\theta_2)\in[-\pi,\pi]^2}\frac{f(\theta_1,\theta_2)}{2-cos\theta_1-cos\theta_2}>0,\)

then the BTTB matrix \(T_n^{(2)}(f)\) generated by _f_ satisfies the following property: there exists a constant \(\beta\) such that (3.8) holds, where the prolongation operator \(I_H^h\) is defined by (3.3).

**Theorem 3.2.** Let \(A_N=T_n^{(2)}\left(f_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(\theta_1,\theta_2)\right)\), where \(f_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(\theta_1,\theta_2)\) is given by (2.7). Let \(I_H^h\) be defined as in (3.3). Then there exists a constant \(\beta\) satisfying (3.8) in Lemma 3.1.

_Proof._ Set<tbc>

\(\eta=\frac{\eta_2}{\eta_1}>0.\)

<tbc>Define<tbc>

\(\phi_\gamma(\theta)=|\theta|\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{2}\right)+\frac{\gamma\pi}{2}.\)

<tbc>Since \(|\theta|\in[0,\pi]\) and \(\gamma\in(1,2)\), we have<tbc>

\(-cos\left(\phi_\gamma(\theta)\right)\ge-cos(\frac{\gamma\pi}{2})\)

<tbc>and<tbc>

\(\left(sin\frac{|\theta|}{2}\right)^\gamma\ge sin^2\frac{|\theta|}{2}=\frac{(1-cos\theta)}{2}.\)

<tbc>Then

\(f_\gamma(\theta)=-2^{\gamma+1}\left(sin\frac{|\theta|}{2}\right)^\gamma cos\left(\phi_\gamma(\theta)\right)\)<br>
\(\ge-2^\gamma cos\left(\frac{\gamma\pi}{2}\right)(1-cos\theta)\)

Consequently, for every \((\theta_1,\theta_2)\neq(0,0)\),

\(f_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(\theta_1,\theta_2)=f_{\gamma_1}(\theta_1)+\eta f_{\gamma_2}(\theta_2)\)<br>
\(\ge-2^{\gamma_1}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_1\pi}{2}\right)(1-cos\theta_1)-\eta 2^{\gamma_2}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_2\pi}{2}\right)(1-cos\theta_2)\)<br>
\(\ge min\left\{-2^{\gamma_1}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_1\pi}{2}\right),-\eta 2^{\gamma_2}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_2\pi}{2}\right)\right\}(2-cos\theta_1-cos\theta_2).\)

Hence,<tbc>

\(\frac{f_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(\theta_1,\theta_2)}{2-cos\theta_1-cos\theta_2}\ge min\left\{-2^{\gamma_1}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_1\pi}{2}\right),-\eta 2^{\gamma_2}\left(cos\frac{\gamma_2\pi}{2}\right)\right\}>0.\)

<tbc>Therefore,<tbc>

\(min_{(\theta_1,\theta_2)\in[-\pi,\pi](\theta_1,\theta_2)\neq(0,0)}^2\frac{f_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(\theta_1,\theta_2)}{2-cos\theta_1-cos\theta_2}>0.\)
