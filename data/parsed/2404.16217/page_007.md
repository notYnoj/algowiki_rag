\(\blacktriangleright\) **Lemma 13** (Locality Lemma for Flow Preservers)**.** _Let_ A _be an algorithm that given any graph_ G _and any vertex_ \(v\in V(\mathcal{G})\)_, computes a_ \((\lambda,k)\)_-FT-BFP of_ G _with at most_ \(c_{\lambda,k}\) _in-edges to v. Then using_ A_, one can construct for any n vertex digraph a_ \((\lambda,k)\)_-FT-BFP with at most_ \(c_{\lambda,k}\cdot n\) _edges._

**Proof.** Consider a graph G with _n_ vertices, namely, _v_<sub>1</sub>_,...,v<sub>n</sub>_. We will provide a construction of \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G using black-box access to algorithm A. We compute a sequence of graphs G<sub>0</sub>,G<sub>1</sub>_,...,_G_<sub>n</sub>_ as follows:

1. Initialize G<sub>0</sub>=G. 2. For \(i\ge 1\), compute G_<sub>i</sub>_ in two steps: **a.** First use A to compute a \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G<sub>_i_-1</sub> in which the in-degree of _v<sub>i</sub>_ is bounded by \(c_{\lambda,k}\), let this graph be H<sub>_i_-1</sub>. **b.** Obtain G_<sub>i</sub>_ from G<sub>_i_-1</sub> by restricting the incoming edges of _v<sub>i</sub>_ to those present in H<sub>_i_-1</sub>. It is easy to verify that the in-degree of each vertex in G_<sub>n</sub>_ is at most \(c_{\lambda,k}\). To show that G_<sub>n</sub>_ is a \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G, it suffices to show that G_<sub>i</sub>_ is a \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G<sub>_i_-1</sub>, for each \(i\ge 1\). Let us fix an index _i_ in the range [1_,n_]. Consider a set _F_ of at most _k_ edges in G<sub>_i_-1</sub>, and let \(\alpha=min\big(\lambda,\text{max-flow}(s,v_i,\mathcal{G}_{i-1}\backslash F)\big).\) By construction, H<sub>_i_-1</sub> is a \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G<sub>_i_-1</sub>, so there exists at least \(\alpha\) edge-disjoint paths from _s_ to _v<sub>i</sub>_ in the graph H<sub>_i_-1</sub>\\_F_. Let E_<sub>i</sub>_ be the set of in-edges of _v<sub>i</sub>_ corresponding to these \(\alpha\) edge-disjoint paths. Observe that the edges in E_<sub>i</sub>_ lie in graph G_<sub>i</sub>_\\_F_. Moreover, graphs G_<sub>i</sub>_\\_F_ and G<sub>_i_-1</sub>\\_F_ differ only at in-edges of _v<sub>i</sub>_. Therefore by Lemma 12 it follows that for any vertex \(t\in V(G)\), \(\text{max-flow}(s,t,\mathcal{G}_i\backslash F)\ge min\big(\lambda,\text{max-flow}(s,t,\mathcal{G}_{i-1}\backslash F)\big)\). This proves that G_<sub>i</sub>_ is a \((\lambda,k)\)-FT-BFP of G<sub>_i_-1</sub>. \(\blacktriangleleft\)
