### A.2 Exact Active-Neighbor Special Case

Although strong connectivity and navigability do not require \(\mathcal{H}_\ell(c)\) to match an exact _j_-nearest-neighbor graph edge-for-edge, our recovery operator reproduces the exact active _j_-NN neighborhood whenever the row contains those neighbors. Specifically, let \(N_j^\star(u,c)\) denote the exact top-_j_ active vector neighbors of _u_ in A(_c_)\\{_u_} ordered by \(\prec_u\). Then

\(N_j^\star(u,c)\subseteq\mathrm{row}(u)\Longleftrightarrow N_{\mathcal{H}_j(c)}^+(u)=N_j^\star(u,c).\) (4)

To verify (4), observe that both row(_u_) and \(N_j^\star(u,c)\) are ordered by the same strict total order \(v\prec_uw\Longleftrightarrow(d(x_u,x_v),v)\prec_\mathrm{lex}(d(x_u,x_w),w)\). If \(N_j^\star(u,c)\subseteq\mathrm{row}(u)\), then removing inactive entries from row(_u_) leaves _C<sub>u</sub>(c_) as a subsequence of \(\prec_u\) that contains the globally smallest min{_j,m_-1} elements of A(_c_)\\{_u_}; hence its prefix of length min{_j,m_-1} is precisely \(N_j^\star(u,c)\). The converse holds because every edge of H_<sub>j</sub>(c_) at _u_ is drawn from row(_u_).
