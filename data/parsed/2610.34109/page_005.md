## 4 The Proposed REACH Index

To realize the guarantees of Section 3 in a single fixed-width graph, REACH stores one row row(_u_) of width _B=B<sub>v</sub>+B<sub>g</sub>_ for each vertex \(u\in V\) . Each row combines a _global-vector source_ of quota _B<sub>v</sub>_ (which provides long-range vector shortcuts on broad disks where \(\rho_c<\mathrm{diam}(\Omega_c)\)) with a _fiber-aware geographic stream_ of quota _B<sub>g</sub>_ (which provides local connectivity on selective disks under Theorem 3.7), and sorts all retained neighbors by \(d(x_u,\cdot)\).

### 4.1 Dual-Source Row Construction

Algorithm 1 constructs row(_u_) for each vertex \(u\in V\) in three stages (Figure 3). In **Stage 1 (Lines 1-4)**, NN-Descent \[11\] builds an approximate _K_<sub>vec</sub>-NN candidate pool _C<sub>v</sub>(u_) in X; after deduplicating _C<sub>v</sub>(u_) and sorting it by exact float32 distance under \(\prec_u\), lines 2-4 apply \(\alpha\)-pruning and back-fill up to quota _B<sub>v</sub>_. In **Stage 2 (Lines 5-11)**, each coordinate \(s=(\lambda,\phi)\) is mapped to its 3D unit vector \(\mathbf{u}_s=(\mathrm{cos}\phi\mathrm{cos}\lambda,\mathrm{cos}\phi\mathrm{sin}\lambda,\mathrm{sin}\phi)\). Because squared chord distance \(\|\mathbf{u}_s-\mathbf{u}_t\|_2^2=2-2\langle\mathbf{u}_s,\mathbf{u}_t\rangle\) is strictly monotone in \(\Delta(s,t)\), grouping identical unit vectors forms the fibers {_F(z_<sub>1</sub>)_,...,F(z<sub>M</sub>_)}, and a 3D kd-tree \[5\] streams fibers in exact great-circle order from _s<sub>u</sub>_. For each fiber _F(z_), line 6 resets the co-located witness list \(W\leftarrow[]\), and lines 7-11 scan up to _C_<sub>tie</sub> members in ascending \(d(x_u,\cdot)\): if an earlier witness \(w\in W\) satisfies \(d(x_v,x_w)\le d(x_u,x_v)/\alpha\), candidate _v_ is pruned (line 9); otherwise _v_ enters _W_ (line 10), and if \(v\notin P\), it is appended to _P_ and increments _g_ (line 11).
