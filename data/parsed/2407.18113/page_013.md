<tbc>the window are different with really high probability, and it is not hard to verify that the lower bounds that this technique can establish then behave in _O_(1_/k_). On the other hand, it is known that \(\gamma_k\) behaves in \(2/\sqrt{k}+o(1/\sqrt{k})\) [13]. It is claimed in [2] that [6] contains a proof of \(\gamma_k\ge 1/\sqrt{k}\), but the only result in [6] that resembles this is \(\gamma_k\ge\frac{2k^2}{k^3+2k-1}\) . An explicit lower bound in \(\Theta(\sqrt{k})\) can probably be adapted from the asymptotic lower bound of [9]. Assuming that the claimed lower bound of \(\gamma_k\ge 1/\sqrt{k}\) is true, it is a better bound that what can be done with our software on modern machines for any \(k\ge k_0\) for some _k_<sub>0</sub> between 50 and 100.
