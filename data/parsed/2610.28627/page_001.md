Matrix Multiplication from Polynomial Packings: A Constructive and Elementary Proof of \(\omega\le 9/4\)

Rafael Pass<sup>∗</sup>

**Abstract**

We give a self-contained constructive proof that the matrix multiplication exponent satisfies \(\omega\le 9/4\). This bound was recently claimed by OpenAI [Ope26a] through non-constructive arguments in Strassen’s asymptotic spectral framework, counting exact scalar arithmetic operations over the complex numbers. We explicitly construct a Strassen-style matrix multiplication identity and obtain the bound by ordinary block recursion. For every \(\gamma>0\), the resulting algorithm multiplies two \(n\times n\) integer matrices using \(O(n^{9/4+\gamma})\) integer arithmetic operations.
