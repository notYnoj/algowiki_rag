## 24 Asteroidal Triples and Friends in (Likely Necessary) Matrix Multiplication Time

For _k\>_3, we note that the graph **H** can still be constructed following Lemma 4.15. In this case, however, we augment **H** by adding a path of length _k_-3 connecting a new auxiliary node \(u'\) to _u_. We then verify the existence of a \(u'v\)-trail. This construction remains valid because any trail from \(u'\) to _v_ must necessarily pass through _u_, and the appended path segment is chordless.
