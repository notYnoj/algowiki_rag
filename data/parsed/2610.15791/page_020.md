<tbc>Finally \((\alpha/\beta)\rho^2=mR\). The range of the first block of \(C_\alpha E_1C_\alpha\) is the kernel of the first slack block, spanned by \((1,-\rho)^\mathsf{T}\). The range of each repeated block is the kernel of the second slack block, spanned by \((\gamma,1)^\mathsf{T}\). So \(\mathrm{tr}[(\mathcal{L}-M_1)C_\alpha E_1C_\alpha]=0\), and by covariance the same holds for every _i_. The average success is

\(\sum_i\mathrm{tr}(M_iC_\alpha E_iC_\alpha)=\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{L}C_\alpha^2)=\alpha Q+N\beta Q=Q,\)

and covariance of the state family makes the success equal to _Q_ on every order.

The closed forms in Corollary 2 follow from the same cubic. For _r_=1, _F_<sub>_n,_1</sub>(_z_)=2_k(z_+1)\[_nz_<sup>2</sup>+(3-_n)z_-3_m_\], whose positive root \((n-3+\sqrt{13n^2-18n+9})/(2n)\) gives the first closed form. For _r=n/_2, _h_=0 gives the second closed form.
