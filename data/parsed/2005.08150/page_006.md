**Proposition 1** _Let_ \(\mu\) _and_ \(\mu'\) _denote two matchings in G such that_ \(\mu\) _is stable and_ \(\mu'\) _is not. Then, for each blocking edge with respect to_ \(\mu'\) _we know that at least one of the endpoints has different matching partners in_ \(\mu\) _and_ \(\mu'\).

_Proof._ Let _uv_ be a blocking edge with respect to \(\mu'\). Towards the contrary, suppose that \(\mu'(u)=\mu(u)\) and \(\mu'(v)=\mu(v)\). Since _uv_ is a blocking edge with respect to \(\mu'\), we have that \(v\succ_u\mu'(u)\), and \(u\succ_v\mu'(v)\). Therefore, \(v\succ_u\mu(u)\), and \(u\succ_v\mu(v)\). Hence, _uv_ is also a blocking edge with respect to \(\mu\), a contradiction to that \(\mu\) is a stable matching in _G_.
