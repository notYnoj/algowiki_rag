Let \(A,B\in\mathbb{F}^{r\times m}\) be the representation matrices where the _i_-th column of _A_ corresponds to _a<sub>i</sub>_ and the _i_-th column of _B_ corresponds to _b<sub>i</sub>_. Let \(D\in\mathbb{F}_{2W}[Y]^{m\times m}\) be a diagonal matrix with _D<sub>i,i</sub>=x<sub>i</sub>Y_<sup>_W+wt(i_)</sup>, where _x<sub>i</sub>_ are independent uniformly random variables.

We define the \(r\times r\) skew-symmetric LMP matrix as:<tbc>

\(\mathcal{M}_{LMP}:=ADB^T-BDA^T\)

<tbc>**Theorem 6.1** (Enumerating Parity Bases \[Lov80, CGM92\])**.** _If the linear matroid M contains at least one parity basis, then the determinant of_ M_<sub>LMP</sub> enumerates the parity bases of M. Specifically,_

\(\mathrm{det}(\mathcal{M}_{LMP})=Y^{rW}\left(\sum_{S\subseteq[m]|S|=r/2}\mathrm{det}(M_S)\big(\prod_{i\in S}x_i\big)Y^{wt(S)}\right)^2\)

_where M<sub>S</sub> is the_ \(r\times r\) _submatrix formed by interleaving the columns a<sub>i</sub> and b<sub>i</sub> for all_ \(i\in S\)_. Furthermore, with high probability, the minimum degree of Y in_ det(M_<sub>LMP</sub>_) _minus rW equals exactly twice the weight of the minimum-weight parity basis._

_Proof._ Because M_<sub>LMP</sub>_ is a skew-symmetric matrix of even dimension _r_, its determinant is the square of a polynomial known as its Pfaffian, i.e., det(M_<sub>LMP</sub>_)=Pf(M_<sub>LMP</sub>_)<sup>2</sup>.

The Pfaffian of the matrix \(\sum_{i=1}^mx_iY^{W+wt(i)}(a_ib_i^T-b_ia_i^T)\) can be explicitly expanded as the sum of the determinants of all \(r\times r\) minors generated by subsets of size _r/_2. Specifically,

\(\mathrm{Pf}(\mathcal{M}_{LMP})=\sum_{S\subseteq[m]|S|=r/2}\mathrm{det}(M_S)\prod_{i\in S}\left(x_iY^{W+wt(a_i,b_i)}\right)\)

where _M<sub>S</sub>_ is the \(r\times r\) matrix whose columns are exactly the pairs {_a<sub>i</sub>,b<sub>i</sub>_} for \(i\in S\).

Observe that \(\mathrm{det}(M_S)\neq 0\) if and only if the columns of _M<sub>S</sub>_ are linearly independent over F. Because _M<sub>S</sub>_ contains exactly _r_ columns corresponding to _r/_2 pairs, this condition holds if and only if _S_ is a valid parity basis. Factoring out the base weight _W_ from the exponent yields:

\(\mathrm{Pf}(\mathcal{M}_{LMP})=Y^\frac{r}{2}W\sum_{S\text{is a parity basis}}\pm\mathrm{det}(M_S)\big(\prod_{i\in S}x_i\big)Y^{wt(S)}\)

Squaring the Pfaffian to obtain the determinant directly produces the formula stated in the theorem.

Let _S_<sup>∗</sup> be an optimal basis. Then the coefficient of \(Y^\frac{r}{2}W+wt(S^*)\) in Pf(M_<sub>LMP</sub>_) is a polynomial of degree _r/_2 in the variables _x<sub>i</sub>_; it is not identically zero since the monomials \(\prod_{i\in S}x_i\) of distinct minimum-weight parity bases _S_ are distinct. By the Schwartz-Zippel lemma, its evaluation at independent uniform _x<sub>i</sub>_ from a field of size \(\Omega(\text{poly}(m))\) is nonzero with high probability. Since the Pfaffian is squared, the term corresponding to optimal basis contributes a _Y_ -exponent of \(2(\frac{r}{2}W+wt(S^*))=rW+2wt(S^*)\). Therefore, isolating the smallest exponent of _Y_ in det(M_<sub>LMP</sub>_) and subtracting _rW_ yields exactly 2_wt(S_<sup>∗</sup>). □
