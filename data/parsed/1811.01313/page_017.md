Figure 2: Construction of the coding network _G_<sup>∗</sup> from the I/O graph _G_. Figure (a) shows the I/O graph _G_ for an array _A_ consisting of 3-bit strings \(d_1,\cdots,d_8\) with _w_=3 and _B_=2. Figure (b) shows the coding network _G_<sup>∗</sup> derived from _G_. All pink edges have the capacity of _w_ bits and all blue edges have the capacity of _b_ bits. Capacity of other edges are specified by their labels. In this example we assume that \(\pi(7)=1\) and \(\pi(5)=2\). Therefore, the output block containing \(C[\pi(7)]\) and \(C[\pi(5)]\) is the first output block which is the node 9, and we added an edge with the capacity _w_ from the node 9 to the sinks _t_<sub>7</sub> and _t_<sub>5</sub>.
