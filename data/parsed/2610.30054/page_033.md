**Definition 5.2.** We define a set F of formal expressions, and three shorthand symbols for expressions in F.

(i) Let F be the set of formal expressions generated by the symbols P_<sub>x,y,z</sub>_, for all admissible triples (_x,y,z_) of positive integers, under \(\oplus\), \(\otimes\), \(\oplus_1\), \(\oplus_2\) and \(\oplus_3\), where \(S\oplus_iT\) is allowed only if the _i_th legs of _S_ and _T_ have the same dimension.<tbc>

(ii) Let \(\langle 1\rangle:=\mathsf{P}_{1,1,1}\).<tbc>

(iii) For every positive integer _r_, let \(\langle r\rangle\) be the direct sum of _r_ copies of \(\langle 1\rangle\).<tbc>

(iv) For all positive integers _n_<sub>1</sub>, _n_<sub>2</sub> and _n_<sub>3</sub>, let \(\langle n_1,n_2,n_3\rangle:=\mathsf{P}_{n_1,1,n_1}\otimes\mathsf{P}_{n_2,n_2,1}\otimes\mathsf{P}_{1,n_3,n_3}\).

Every expression in F represents a tensor, obtained by reading P_<sub>x,y,z</sub>_ as the tensor of Definition 5.1 and recursively evaluating \(\oplus\), \(\otimes\) and \(\oplus_i\) as the direct sum, the tensor product and the partial direct sums, which are defined analogously to \(\oplus_1\). Distinct formal expressions may represent the same tensor.

Note that indeed the expressions \(\langle r\rangle\) and \(\langle n_1,n_2,n_3\rangle\) represent the unit tensors and, up to isomorphism, the matrix multiplication tensors, since \(\mathsf{P}_{n,1,n}\cong\langle n,1,1\rangle\), \(\mathsf{P}_{n,n,1}\cong\langle 1,n,1\rangle\) and \(\mathsf{P}_{1,n,n}\cong\langle 1,1,n\rangle\) and \(\langle n_1,n_2,n_3\rangle=\langle n_1,1,1\rangle\otimes\langle 1,n_2,1\rangle\otimes\langle 1,1,n_3\rangle\).
