### Concurrent work.

OpenAI released a proof that \(\omega_\mathbb{C}\le 9/4\), with a formal proof in Lean, on 6 October 2026 [Ope26a]. Since then, Sela Navot, Kunal Marwaha and we each released a Lean proof that \(\omega_\mathbb{F}\le 9/4\) for every field F (7 October; `selanavot/matrix-multiplicatio n-all-fields` on GitHub, `mme\_omegaga\_le\_225` on Prove2Me, and `jzuiddam/omegaga-nine-q uarters-all-fields` on GitHub). Lü released a proof that \(\omega_\mathbb{F}\le 9/4\) for every field F [Lü26] (12 October). Uznański released proofs of the bounds of Corollary 4.17, and then of those of Theorem 1.2, in characteristic zero [Uzn26] (7 and 11 October), and, assuming Strassen’s spectral theorem, a Lean proof of them for every field (12 October). Zhou and Wang released a Lean proof that the bounds of Corollary 4.17 hold over every associative ring (14 October; on GitHub, `Hugin-Hilbert/rectangular-matmul-rings`). The authors of two GitHub repositories, `eliottcassidy2000/math` and `acprk/openai-math-followups`, released proofs that, for square matrices, the kind of analysis we do in Section 4 is optimal, using a function similar to _F<sup>⋆</sup>_ of (5.3) (7 and 10 October); our operational barrier Theorem 5.4 is stronger as it applies to any combination of the degeneration ingredients, and applies to all rectangular shapes.
