**Lemma 9** _There is a binary alphabetic code_ C<sup>2</sup>_, such that (1) for t_=0_, ..., W_-1 _the codeword length of a symbol S_\[_t_+1\]=_a<sub>i</sub> in_ C<sup>2</sup> _does not exceed_ min(log(_t/w<sub>i</sub>(t_)),log_n_)+_O_(1) _(2) the cost of updating_ C<sup>2</sup> _after encoding a symbol or inserting a new symbol into_ C<sup>2</sup> _is O_((loglog_n_)<sup>3</sup>).

_Proof_: We divide the tree _T_<sup>S</sup> into the macro-tree and mini-trees. Every mini-tree has \(\Theta(log^4n)\) pseudo-leaves. We keep codewords for all nodes \(\varepsilon_i\) in the macro-tree. We also keep codewords for the roots of all mini-trees. Finally we also store mini-codewords for nodes \(\varepsilon_j\) stored in mini-trees. The mini-codeword of a node \(\varepsilon_j\) in a mini-tree \(T_g^\mathrm{S}\) encodes the path from the root of \(T_g^\mathrm{S}\) to \(\varepsilon_j\). We maintain mini-codewords using Lemma 8. When the number of pseudo-leaves in a mini-tree equals 2log<sup>4</sup>_n_, we split the mini-tree into two mini-trees of equal size. Then we insert a new leaf into the macro-tree and update it in _O_(log<sup>3</sup>_n_) time. The total time needed to update the macro-tree, codewords of macro-leaves, and codewords of nodes in the macro-tree is _O_(log<sup>3</sup>_n_). Hence the total amortized cost of inserting a pseudo-leaf is _O_((loglog_n_)<sup>3</sup>).

For every symbol _a<sub>j</sub>_ we record the node \(\varepsilon_j\) that holds _a<sub>j</sub>_; if \(\varepsilon_j\) is stored in a mini-tree _T<sub>g</sub>_, then the node \(\varepsilon_j\) also contains a pointer to _T<sub>g</sub>_. In order to produce the codeword for the next symbol _S_\[_t_+1\]=_a<sub>i</sub>_, we look up the node \(\varepsilon_i\) holding _a<sub>i</sub>_. If \(\varepsilon_i\) is in the macro-tree, we output its codeword. If \(\varepsilon_i\) is in a mini-tree _T<sub>g</sub>_, the codeword of _a<sub>i</sub>_ is obtained by concatenating the codeword of _T<sub>g</sub>_ and the mini-codeword of \(\varepsilon_i\).
