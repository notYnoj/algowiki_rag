By symmetry, we also have:

\(\frac{|\Omega^{\mathcal{C}\backslash\{c_0\}}|}{|\Omega^\mathcal{C}|}\ge\left(1-2\cdot 2^{-M}\right)\frac{\sum_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)\in\mathcal{L}_\mathrm{coup}}|\Omega^{\mathcal{E}\wedge\sigma}|\cdot\hat{p}_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)}^X}{\sum_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)\in\mathcal{L}_\mathrm{coup}}|\Omega^{\mathcal{F}\wedge\tau}|\cdot\hat{p}_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)}^Y}\)<br>
\(\ge\left(1-2\cdot 2^{-M}\right)r_-.\)

Combining these results, we conclude:

\(\left(1-2\cdot 2^{-M}\right)r_-\le\frac{|\Omega^{\mathcal{C}\backslash\{c_0\}}|}{|\Omega^\mathcal{C}|}\le\left(1+2\cdot 2^{-M}\right)r_+.\) □

## 5.3.2. _Proof of the average marginal bound (Claim 5.11)._

To prove Claim 5.11, we introduce an auxiliary random process induced by the LP feasible solution. This random process plays a key role in the proof of Claim 5.11, and also forms the foundation for the sampling algorithm derived from the LP.

**Definition 5.12** (random path induced by the LP solution)**.** We define the following random process for generating a random path from the root to a leaf in the _M_-truncated coupling tree \(\mathcal{T}=\mathcal{T}_M(\Phi,c_0)\), based on a feasible solution \(\hat{p}_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)}^X\), \(\hat{p}_{(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{F},\sigma,\tau,T,\varsigma)}^Y\) to the linear program in Definition 5.6.

(1) If \(\mathcal{F}^\tau\unknown\subseteq\mathcal{E}^\sigma\), let _c_ be the smallest constraint in \(\mathcal{F}^\tau\backslash\mathcal{E}^\sigma\):<tbc>
