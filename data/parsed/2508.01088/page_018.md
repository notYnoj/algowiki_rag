**Remark 3.14.** _The vector_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^2\) _is the sum of four vectors:_

• \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,1}\) _- a vector whose_ \(n-\lambda-2^{th}\) _column is_ \(2T(n-2\lambda-6)\) _and the remaining entries are zero;_<tbc>

• \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,2}\) _whose non null components are_ \(-n+2\lambda+4\) _and they form a triangular shape limited by conditions_ \(i=n-\lambda-2\)_, j_=\(n-\lambda-3\) _and_ \(i-j=n-\lambda-3\);<tbc>

• \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,3}\) _whose non null components are_ \(-n+2\lambda+4\) _and they form a triangular shape limited by conditions_ \(i=\lambda+3\), \(j=n-\lambda-3\) _and_ \(i-j=\lambda+2\);<tbc>

• \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,4}\) _whose non null components are_ \(-n+2\lambda+4\) _and, when_ \(2(n-\lambda-3)<\lambda+3\)_, they form a triangular shape limited by conditions_ \(i=\lambda+3\), \(j=\lambda+2\) _and_ \(i-j=n-\lambda-3\)_, when_ \(2(n-\lambda-3)\ge\lambda+3\)_, they are limited by conditions_ \(i=\lambda+3\), \(j=n-\lambda-2\)_, j_=\(\lambda+2\) _and_ \(i-j=n-\lambda-3\).

_Note that the_ \(2(n-\lambda-3)^{th}\) _and the_ \(\lambda+3^{th}\) _rows are the last row of_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,2}\) _and first row of_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,3}\) _with non-null components, respectively. Furthermore, when_ \(2n-2\lambda-6<\lambda+2\)_, none of these four vectors have non-zero entries in common. When_ \(2n-2\lambda-6=\lambda+2\) _the vectors_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,1}\) _and_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,4}\) _have one non-null entry in common. And finally, when_ \(2n-2\lambda-6\ge\lambda+3\)_, the vectors_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,1}\) _and_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,4}\)_, and the vectors_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,2}\) _and_ \(y_{n,\lambda}^{2,3}\) _have some non-null components in common._
