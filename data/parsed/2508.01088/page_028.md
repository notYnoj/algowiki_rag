**Example 4.6.** _Considering the graph associated to the chessboard of Example 4.3, let us apply Theorem 4.5 starting with i_<sub>1</sub>=3_, i_<sub>2</sub>=1 _and i_<sub>3</sub>=_j_<sub>3</sub>=4.

\(\underbrace{\lambda_{i_3+j_3-1}}_{\lambda_{j_2}=\lambda_7}\left(\underbrace{A(G_{3,H}^2)+A(G_{3,V}^2)}_{A(G_3^2)}\right)\le\underbrace{\lambda_4(G_{3,H}^2)}_0+\underbrace{\lambda_4(G_{3,V}^2)}_0\)<br>
\(\underbrace{\lambda_{i_2+j_2-1}}_{\lambda_{j_1}=\lambda_7}\left(\underbrace{A(G_3^1)+A(G_3^2)}_{A(G_3)}\right)\le\underbrace{\lambda_1(G_3^1)}_3+\lambda_7(G_3^2)\le 3+0\)<br>
\(\underbrace{\lambda_{i_1+j_1-1}}_{\lambda_9}\left(\underbrace{A(G_{1,2})+A(G_3)}_{\mathcal{Q}(4)}\right)\le\underbrace{\lambda_3(G_{1,2})}_1+\lambda_7(G_3)\le 1+3.\)

_On the other hand, starting with r_<sub>1</sub>=15_, r_<sub>2</sub>=16 _and r_<sub>3</sub>=_s_<sub>3</sub>=13_, we obtain_

\(\underbrace{\lambda_{r_3+s_3-16}}_{\lambda_{s_2}=\lambda_{10}}\left(\underbrace{A(G_{3,H}^2)+A(G_{3,V}^2)}_{A(G_3^2)}\right)\ge\underbrace{\lambda_{13}(G_{3,H}^2)}_0+\underbrace{\lambda_{13}(G_{3,V}^2)}_0\)<br>
\(\underbrace{\lambda_{r_2+s_2-16}}_{\lambda_{s_1}=\lambda_{10}}\left(\underbrace{A(G_3^1)+A(G_3^2)}_{A(G_3)}\right)\ge\underbrace{\lambda_{16}(G_3^1)}_{-1}+\lambda_{10}(G_3^2)\ge-1+0\)<br>
\(\underbrace{\lambda_{r_1+s_1-16}}_{\lambda_9}\left(\underbrace{A(G_{1,2})+A(G_3)}_{\mathcal{Q}(4)}\right)\ge\underbrace{\lambda_{15}(G_{1,2})}_{-2}+\lambda_{10}(G_3)\ge-2-1.\)

_Therefore, we may conclude that_ \(-3\le\lambda_9(\mathcal{Q}(4))\le 4\)_. It should be observed that the eigenvalues are organized in non-increasing order, that is,_ \(\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_{42}\).
