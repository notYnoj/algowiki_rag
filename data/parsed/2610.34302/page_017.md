<tbc>Combining the above inequalities, we get

\(|C|<0.21lnn\cdot 1.1|C^\star|<\frac{1}{2}lnn\cdot|C^\star|,\)

contradicting Lemma 3.1. Thus, for \(c_\eta=1/(10d)\), no polynomial-time \((\alpha,2\alpha-\eta)\)-bicriteria \(c_\eta lnN\)- approximation exists unless P=NP. \(\square\)
