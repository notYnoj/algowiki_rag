Every marked pair causes the relaxation of 2_n_ pairs when extracted from _Q_. Additionally, it can be relaxed by at most 2_n_ unmarked pairs when they are extracted from _Q_. A marked pair is hence responsible for O(_n_) cost during the whole algorithm. All extractions from _Q_ cost O(_n_<sup>2</sup>log_n_) using Fibonacci heaps [24]. Therefore, the main part of the algorithm takes time \(\mathcal{O}(\eta(G,C,p)\cdot n+n^2logn)\). The distance estimate and verification parts take time O(_n_<sup>2</sup>(_q_+log_n_)+_n_<sup>3.66</sup>_/p_) by Lemmas 3 and 4. In total, this gives the final running time.
