In the following proposition, we will have \(\alpha^{(r)},\beta_{X,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Y,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Z,i,j,k}^{(r)}\) for every \(r\in[3]\). For every \(r\in[3]\), we use superscript (_r_) on variables to denote that they are computed using values of \(\alpha^{(r)},\beta_{X,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Y,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Z,i,j,k}^{(r)}\).

**Proposition 5.1.** \(\big(\mathrm{CW}_q^{\otimes 2^{\ell-1}}\big)^{\otimes n}\) _can be degenerated into_<tbc>

\(2^{(A_1E_1+A_2E_2+A_3E_3)n-o(n)}\)

<tbc>_independent copies of a level-_\(\ell\) _interface tensor with parameter list_

\(\big\{\big(n\cdot A_r\cdot\alpha^{(r)}(i,j,k),i,j,k,\beta_{X,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Y,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Z,i,j,k}^{(r)}\big)\big\}_{r\in[3],i+j+k=2^\ell}\)

_where_

• \(0\le A_1,A_2,A_3\le 1,A_1+A_2+A_3=1\);<tbc>

• \(\alpha^{(r)}\) _for every_ \(r\in[3]\) _is a distribution over_ \(\{(i,j,k)\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^3|i+j+k=2^\ell\}\);<tbc>

• _For every_ \(W\in\{X,Y,Z\}\), \(\beta_{W,i,j,k}^{(r)}\) _for_ \(r\in[3],i+j+k=2^{\ell-1}\) _is a level-_\(\ell\) _complete split distribution;_<tbc>

• \(E_1:=\mathrm{min}\big\{H(\alpha_X^{(1)})-P_\alpha^{(1)},H(\alpha_Y^{(1)})-P_\alpha^{(1)},H(\overline{\beta_{Z,*,*,*}^{(1)}})-\lambda_Z^{(1)}\big\},\)<tbc>

<tbc>\(E_2:=\mathrm{min}\big\{H(\alpha_X^{(2)})-P_\alpha^{(2)},H(\alpha_Z^{(2)})-P_\alpha^{(2)},H(\overline{\beta_{Y,*,*,*}^{(2)}})-\lambda_Y^{(2)}\big\},\)<tbc>

<tbc>\(E_3:=\mathrm{min}\big\{H(\alpha_Y^{(3)})-P_\alpha^{(3)},H(\alpha_Z^{(3)})-P_\alpha^{(3)},H(\overline{\beta_{X,*,*,*}^{(3)}})-\lambda_X^{(3)}\big\}.\)

<tbc>**Remark 5.2.** Note that without loss of generality, we can assume that, for every _r,i,j,k_, and every \(L\in\{0,1,2\}^{2^{\ell-1}}\),<tbc>

\(\beta_{X,i,0,k}^{(r)}(L)=\beta_{Z,i,0,k}^{(r)}(\vec{2}-L),\beta_{Z,0,j,k}^{(r)}(L)=\beta_{Y,0,j,k}^{(r)}(\vec{2}-L),\beta_{Y,i,j,0}^{(r)}(L)=\beta_{X,i,j,0}^{(r)}(\vec{2}-L),\)

<tbc>where \(\vec{2}\) denotes the length-\((2^{\ell-1})\) vector whose coordinates are all 2, and

\(\beta_{X,i,j,k}^{(r)}(L)=0\text{if}\sum_tL_t\neq i,\beta_{Y,i,j,k}^{(r)}(L)=0\text{if}\sum_tL_t\neq j,\beta_{Z,i,j,k}^{(r)}(L)=0\text{if}\sum_tL_t\neq k,\)

because otherwise, the level-\(\ell\) interface tensor will be the zero tensor and the lemma will follow trivially.

Next, we show Theorem 5.3, which is a corollary of Proposition 5.1.

**Theorem 5.3.** _For any_ \(\varepsilon>0\), 2<sup>_o(n_)</sup> _independent copies of_ \((\mathrm{CW}_q^{\otimes 2^{\ell-1}})^{\otimes n}\) _can be degenerated into_<tbc>

\(2^{(A_1E_1+A_2E_2+A_3E_3-o_{1/\varepsilon}(1))n-o(n)}\)

<tbc>_independent copies of a level-_\(\ell\) \(\varepsilon\)_-interface tensor with parameter list_

\(\big\{\big(n\cdot A_r\cdot\alpha^{(r)}(i,j,k),i,j,k,\beta_{X,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Y,i,j,k}^{(r)},\beta_{Z,i,j,k}^{(r)}\big)\big\}_{r\in[3],i+j+k=2^\ell}\)

_where the constraints are the same as those in Proposition 5.1._<sup>3</sup><tbc>

<sup>3</sup>\(o_{1/\varepsilon}(1)\) denotes a function \(f(\varepsilon)\) where \(f(\varepsilon)\rightarrow 0\) as \(\varepsilon\rightarrow 0\). We also use \(o_{1/\varepsilon}(n)\) to denote \(o_{1/\varepsilon}(1)\cdot n\).
