## 6.2 Empirical Benchmarks on Skewed Data

The results presented in  6 were generated using a skewed dataset of 64-bit integers, representing the ideal use-case for our optimization.

To rigorously evaluate SP-LSD against realistic high-performance workloads without relying on artificial random skew, we utilized a ‘System-Calibrated’ benchmark modeling **Heavy-Tailed Delta-Encoded Streams** (e.g., compressed logs). Furthermore, to strictly enforce the structural requirement of supporting the full 64-bit range (_k\>n_<sup>2</sup>) and prevent naive global-clipping optimizations, we explicitly injected 1% outliers of the maximum 64-bit integer (2<sup>64</sup>-1). This configuration generates a realistic bitwise density profile. Additionally, to ensure benchmark reflects optimal performance, we apply bitwise operations to both BNRS and SP-LSD implementation rather than computationally expensive modulo and division operations. This allows for a direct validation of the SP-LSD algorithm’s adaptive partitioning strategy.
