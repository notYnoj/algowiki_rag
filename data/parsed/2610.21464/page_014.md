## 8. Conclusion

This paper presents a topology-aware topic-level poisoning attack against large-scale HNSW-RAG. The Hub- Satellite topology improves graph-search reachability and retrieval stability through multiple entry points and local cohesion, while entity-claim promotion combines evidence anchoring and entity-claim coupling to promote the target entity across diverse unseen topic queries. Experiments across datasets and knowledge-base sizes demonstrate attack effectiveness and generalization to unseen topic queries, while structural analysis confirms set-level topological cooperation among poisoned documents. Evaluated defenses incur substantial benign-document loss or leave considerable residual attack success, highlighting security risks in HNSW-RAG systems.
