<tbc>\(\frac{(|D|d_\mathrm{max})^{k+1}-1}{|D|d_\mathrm{max}-1}\) by the geometric series. Furthermore, the bound \(\frac{(|D|d_\mathrm{max})^{k+1}-1}{|D|d_\mathrm{max}-1}\le\frac{(|D|d_\mathrm{max})^{k+1}}{|D|d_\mathrm{max}-1}\le 2(|D|d_\mathrm{max})^k=O((|D|d_\mathrm{max})^k)\) applies.

We can construct (_G,w_) in linear time in the size of _I_, so in _O(d_<sub>max</sub>_n_) time. The algorithm by Huang and Kavitha \[33\] runs in \(O(\sqrt{n_*}m_*N\mathrm{log}(n_*^2/m_*)/\mathrm{log}(n_*))\) time, where _n_<sub>∗</sub> is the number of vertices in the graph, _m_<sub>∗</sub> is the number of edges in the graph and _N_ is the maximum edge weight. Thus, given that \(n_*\le n m_*\le d_\mathrm{max}n\) and \(N\le n+2\), this algorithm runs in _O(d_<sub>max</sub>_n_<sup>5/2</sup>) time.

Putting the complexity analysis together, we consider \(O(d_\mathrm{max}^{|D|})\) candidate matchings and, for every candidate matching, we consider _O_((|_D|d_<sub>max</sub>)_<sup>k</sup>_) sets of blocking pairs, leading to at most \(O(d_\mathrm{max}^{|D|}(|D|d_\mathrm{max})^k)=O(|D|^kd_\mathrm{max}^{|D|+k})\) candidate configurations. For each candidate configuration, we construct a maximum-weight matching instance in _O(d_<sub>max</sub>_n_) time and, subsequently, compute a maximum-weight matching in _O(d_<sub>max</sub>_n_<sup>5/2</sup>) time. Hence, we arrive at a final com plexity of \(O(|D|^kd_\mathrm{max}^{|D|+k+1}n^{5/2})\).

We remark that the bipartite restriction _k_-Deviator-Max-smi can be solved slightly faster than stated above (by at least a factor of \(\frac{n}{\mathrm{log}n}\)) by replacing the maximum-weight matching algorithm by Huang and Kavitha \[33\] with the algorithm by Duan and Su \[16\].
