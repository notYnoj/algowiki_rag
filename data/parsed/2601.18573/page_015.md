**Theorem 4.1.** _Let_ \((I=(A,\succ),D)\) _be an instance of k_-Deviator-Max-sri _with n agents and maximum preference list length d_<sub>max</sub>_. Then the problem k_-Deviator-Max-sri _is solvable in_ \(O(|D|^kd_\mathrm{max}^{|D|+k+1}n^{5/2})\) _time._
