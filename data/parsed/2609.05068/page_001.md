**Abstract**

Real-time services, such as VoIP and large-scale neural network training, require strict transmission delay guarantees. While routing under hop constraints is tractable, real-world delays increase sharply with equipment load, typically modeled using the M/M/1 queuing function where delay is inversely proportional to available bandwidth.
