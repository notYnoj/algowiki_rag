edges. For \(|U|\ge 4\), this value is at least |_U_|<sup>2</sup>/8. Thus,<tbc>

\(\sum_{i,j}|\text{lcs}(b'_i,\bar{b}'_j)|\ge d^{1/3}(U^2/8).\)

<tbc>Since there are |_U_|<sup>2</sup> pairs of blocks, the expected size of the longest common subsequence of two randomly selected blocks of \(U'\) and \(\overline{U'}\) is at least

\(\Omega(d^{1/3})=\Omega(n^{(1/2-26\delta-4\zeta-\eta)/3}).\)

Finally, the combination of Lemmas 8.4 and 8.5 implies that the expected length of the longest common subsequence of two random blocks of _U_ and <o>_U_</o> is<tbc>

\(\Omega(n^{(1/2-26\delta-4\zeta-\eta)/3}\cdot n^{-13\delta-2\zeta-\eta}/\mathrm{log}n),\)

<tbc>that is

\(\tilde{\Omega}(n^{1/6-65/3\delta-10/3\zeta-4/3\eta}).\)
