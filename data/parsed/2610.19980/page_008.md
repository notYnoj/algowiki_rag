**Initialization.** Every run starts from fresh pseudorandom geometry rather than an injected certificate, and the random seeds are retained only for provenance and replay. Initialization is problem- andrepresentation-specific. MISR uses integral rectangle coordinates: at grid 8, repaired lower endpoints lie in 0-9 and upper endpoints in 1-10, and the quadratic- program representation draws its initial coefficients from \[-4,4\]. Unit-square instances place congruent squares whose anchors lie in 0-8 at grid 8 and 0-16 at grid 16; the exact-grid representation samples a common integer side from 1 to 4, while the fixed-symmetry representation fixes the side at 2.
