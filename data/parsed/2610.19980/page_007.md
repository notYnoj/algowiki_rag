<tbc>choices for each problem range from direct coordinate encodings to structured encodings that build the target property into generation, from simple coordinate perturbations to exactly scored, structure-aware hill climbs, and from cheap graph or incidence statistics to surrogates that invoke the exact scorer during ranking.  1 lists all 27 choices.

**Components of the best configurations.** We describe in more detail the components that make up the best configuration for each problem.

For MISR, `triangle\_free\_rect` stores a list of integer rectangle coordinates under the constraint that the intersection graph stay triangle-free, that is, no three rectangles share a common point. Rectangles are added one at a time from a pool that mixes rectangles near existing ones, thin horizontal and vertical bars, and unrestricted random rectangles; a candidate is admissible only if its neighbors in the current intersection graph are pairwise non-intersecting, which is exactly what prevents a triangle. Repair deletes a member of any triangle that appears and refills to the minimum size. On this representation, `sequence\_pair\_pivot` (the local search) samples add, replace, nudge, resize, and delete moves with add and replace upweighted, drawing new rectangles from a large triangle-free pool. The surrogate `exact\_lp\_gap\_pressure` calls the full MISR exact scorer for every candidate it ranks and orders the population primarily by the exact clique-LP-to-integer gap \(\alpha^*/\alpha\), breaking ties in favor of a larger triangle- free lower bound. It is the most expensive MISR signal, since each ranking call solves the optimization exactly, same as the verifier.

For unit-square stabbing, `sqstab\_exact\_grid` stores distinct integer lower-left anchor endpoints and one common integer side length. Canonicalization removes translation and duplicates, so the exact critical-line universe is easy to regenerate and audit. `symmetry\_crossover\_hillclimb` is an operator shared by all three problems that makes 25 exactly scored proposals per call, drawn as about 55% one- step shifts, 25% random relocations, 10% pairwise exchanges (swapping the vertical intervals of two objects), and 10% planar symmetries. Every proposal is repaired and exactly scored; acceptance is gated first by a non-triviality requirement (here, a candidate needing at least four lines is preferred) and then by the exact objective, and only non-worsening proposals are kept. `exact\_stab\_gap\_pressure` calls the exact set- cover and fractional-LP scorer for each candidate and ranks primarily by \(\tau/\tau^*\), with small tie-breaks that prefer a larger integer cover, a smaller fractional optimum, and a more spread-out LP support.

## 5.2 Experimental setup

**Experimental design.** For each problem the three components are crossed fully, giving 27 configurations, and the three problems together give 81. Each configuration repeatedly generates or repairs a population in its representation, scores novel candidates with its surrogate, exactly audits the elite at a fixed interval and saves a certificate only when the exact score strictly improves, mutates the elite with its local-search operation, and periodically trains a freshly initialized transformer on the strongest archive entries and samples new candidates from it, with a character _n_-gram sampler filling any shortfall of parseable samples. Every configuration is run once, from freshly generated geometry, with no repeated-seed axis and no warm-started certificate, under an identical 24-hour wall-clock budget. To attribute an effect to a single component we use its _marginal mean_, the average exact score over the 9 configurations that share that component choice. Because there is one run per configuration, these marginals are descriptive summaries rather than estimates with error bars.

**Hardware and run cohorts.** Experiments ran on compute nodes built from AMD EPYC 7742 processors at 2.25 GHz with 128 cores and 480 GB of RAM each (about 3.75 GB per core). Each configuration was dispatched as one array task requesting four cores and 16 GB of RAM.
