<tbc>with uniformly random instances does not help, because random instances are combinatorially monotonous and leave the search in the same region as before. A deliberately structured search space behaves very differently. Working in the staircase formulation, the search reaches a certified unit-square stabbing gap of \(\tau/\tau^*=14/9\approx 1.555\). This exceeds both the best previously known lower bound of 3/2 and the automated search’s best of 20/13 (Section 5). The gain comes from the structure rather than from any injected answer: the levels, the rational measure, and the product rule supply the proof template, and the search optimizes within it.

**The difficulty of producing structure.** The reason such structure has to come from a human is that the obstructions that force a large gap are hard to manufacture automatically. For unit-square stabbing, a neutral coordinate search must invent both the geometry and its proof of hardness at once from raw square positions; the staircase formulation removes that double burden. The same difficulty recurs, often more sharply, elsewhere: for MISR a large gap wants a dense conflict structure, for which a high chromatic number of the intersection graph is a natural proxy, yet rectangle intersection graphs of chromatic number 5 or 6 are rare and delicate to build while random ones are almost always low-chromatic, so neither hand construction nor uniform sampling readily supplies them. Expert structure such as the staircase is valuable precisely because it makes these otherwise- elusive obstructions native to the search space.

**Interpretation.** Modest expert input, supplied precisely where the automated loop stalls, is enough to surpass the best known lower bound for unit-square stabbing, producing a construction that neither the automated search nor the expert reached alone. The same complementarity appears downstream of the search as well as upstream of it: manual post-processing reduces the automated 20/13 witness from twenty squares to eleven without changing its score, and the 3/2 MISR construction extends by hand into an infinite family. In each case, the search supplied a seed that a human could compress or generalize.

## 7 Conclusion and future directions

Extremal constructions are still found largely by hand. We showed that a learning-guided search can find them automatically, provided the representation, local search, and surrogate are designed together: it reaches a gap of 3/2 for MISR, a destroyed fraction of 1/3 for guillotine cutting, and 20/13≈1.54 for unit-square stabbing, the last exceeding the best previously known lower bound; the staircase formulation raises it to 14/9≈1.555. Every construction carries an independently re-verified certificate. Two lessons follow: good configurations are<tbc>

<tbc>isolated in the design space, so no component succeeds alone, and automated search plateaus for a structural rather than a computational reason, since it cannot leave the region it starts in.

Three directions follow from the plateau. An archive organized by structural features rather than by score would retain candidates that score poorly today but carry the seeds of a better construction; composition operators that combine certified obstructions while preserving their ratio would let a small witness be scaled rather than rediscovered; and the exact structure our solvers already compute - LP duals for MISR, incidences for stabbing, cut trees for guillotine hardness - could guide moves directly. The pipeline applies to any extremal question with an exactly computable, certifiable objective [29, 16]. The division of labor that works is across human and machine: the search covers ground that would be tedious by hand, the expert supplies the template it cannot invent.

## 8 Reproducibility and artifacts

The code, certificates, and analysis scripts are archived at `https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23321041` under CC BY 4.0. Every certificate carries a canonical hash, and a verifier independent of the training code recomputes its exact score, so every reported value can be checked directly. The artifact also contains a reduced smoke-test that verifies a small subset of certificates in minutes and a full reproduction script for the main tables.
