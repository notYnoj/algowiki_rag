fast and intuitive but poorly aligned with the recursive objective, since a construction can force an expensive first cut and yet split into two easy subproblems, so a high first-cut cost need not mean a high final destruction fraction. depth\_limited\_dp runs the recursive-cut dynamic program to depth four, optimistically treating every rectangle still present at the depth limit as saved, and ranks by the implied destroyed fraction plus small balance and span bonuses. It sees past the first cut while staying cheaper than the full dynamic program, but misses obstructions that surface deeper than four levels. k\_subset\_nonseparability is the richest guillotine signal: it measures the fraction of majority-size subsets that are not guillotine-separable (exhaustive for small _n_, sampled otherwise) and combines it with the depth-four estimate, a width-six beam-search estimate, and balance and span terms. Its weakness is that sampled majority-subset resistance is not the same as the exact destruction fraction, especially at larger _n_.

## F PatternBoost pseudocode

The loop of Section 4, shown in Figure 2, is summarized below. It is a population-based search: each generation scores the population with the surrogate, exactly audits the elites at a fixed interval, periodically retrains a fresh model on the archive, and refills the population by mutating elites with the configuration’s local-search operator and adding a few random newcomers.<br> `PatternBoost(problem, repr, local, surr):`<br> `pop \<- fresh random instances`<br> `archive \<- {}; best \<- none`<br> `for g in 1...iterations:`<br> `# score population with the surrogate`<br> `scored \<- []`<br> `for x in pop:`<br> `x \<- repair(repr, x)`<br> `if x seen before: continue`<br> `s \<- surrogate(surr, x)`<br> `scored += (s, x); archive += (s, x)`<br> `elites \<- top-k of scored by surrogate`<tbc>

<tbc>`# periodic exact verification`<br> `if g mod exact\_every == 0:`<br> `for x in elites:`<br> `c \<- exact\_score(problem, x)`<br> `if c ok and c.score \> best.score:`<br> `save certificate; best \<- c`<br> `next \<- elites`<br> `# global learning: train a fresh model`<br> `if g mod train\_every == 0 and archive:`<br> `model \<- train\_model(top of archive)`<br> `for y in sample(model):`<br> `y \<- repair(repr, y)`<br> `if y ok and non-trivial:`<br> `next += y`<br> `# local search, then random newcomers`<br> `while |next| \< pop\_size - num\_new:`<br> `p \<- next elite (else random)`<br> `next += mutate(local, p)`<br> `next += num\_new random instances`<br> `pop \<- next[:pop\_size]`<br> `checkpoint periodically`<br> `return best`
