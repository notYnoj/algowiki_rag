## 6 Future work

We introduced two new notions of fair stable matchings for SMI, namely, the regret-equal stable matching and the min-regret sum stable matching. We presented algorithms that are able to compute matchings of these types in polynomial time: _O(d_<sub>0</sub>_nm_) time for the regret-equal stable matching, where _d_<sub>0</sub>=|_d<sub>U</sub>(M_<sub>0</sub>)-_d<sub>W</sub>(M_<sub>0</sub>)|; and _O(d<sub>s</sub>m_) time for the min-regret sum stable matching, where _d<sub>s</sub>=d<sub>U</sub>(M<sub>z</sub>_)-_d<sub>U</sub>(M_<sub>0</sub>). It remains open as to whether these time complexities can be improved.
