We hope that our technique may find many further applications in derandomizing other algorithms in this area, since a number of previous algorithms used randomized partitioning similar to halvers--to name just two examples, Chen, Lian, Mao, and Zhang’s \(\widetilde{O}(n+\sqrt{ut})\)-time subset sum algorithm [CLMZ24b] (where _u_=max(_X_)) and Bringmann, Dürr, and Polak’s knapsack algorithm [BDP24]. We leave the question of their derandomization to future work (these algorithms have other components that may pose additional challenges to derandomize).
