<tbc>such nodes. Because \(\Delta\ge 3\) and \(\delta\le 1/2\), we have

\(N_0=384\Delta^2(\Delta+1)\epsilon^{-2}\big(\mathrm{ln}6+\mathrm{ln}\delta^{-1}\big)\)<br>
\(\le 384\cdot\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2\cdot\frac{4}{2}\cdot(\Delta-1)^3\epsilon^{-2}\left(\frac{\mathrm{ln}6}{\mathrm{ln}2}+1\right)\mathrm{ln}\delta^{-1}\)<br>
\(<6195(\Delta-1)^3\epsilon^{-2}\mathrm{ln}\delta^{-1}.\)

Therefore we can select integers _j_ and _N_ such that \(j\le 2\Delta\epsilon^{-1}\) and \(N\le 6250(\Delta-1)^3\epsilon^{-2}\mathrm{ln}\delta^{-1}\). In total we make at most

\(N+2(\Delta+1)N+3(\Delta-1)^{2j}N\le 4(\Delta-1)^{2j}N\)<br>
\(<25000\epsilon^{-2}(\Delta-1)^{3+4\Delta/\epsilon}\mathrm{ln}\delta^{-1}\)

queries. This completes the proof.
