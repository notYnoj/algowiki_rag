- If \(q'_{(a,b)}>0\), then \(\alpha'_a+\alpha'_b=0=\mathsf{wt}_{q^\prime}(a,b)\). So _a_ is matched in _M_<sub>1</sub> either to _b_ or to a neighbor preferred to _b_. Hence, \(\mathsf{wt}_{M_1}(a,b)\le 0=\beta_a+\beta_b\).<tbc>

**-** Suppose \(q'_{(a,b)}=0\). Since \(\mathsf{wt}_{q^\prime}(a,b)\le 0\), either (i) one of _a,b_ prefers both its partners in \(q'\) to the other or (ii) both _a,b_ like their more preferred partner in \(q'\) to each other. In both cases, one of _a,b_ is matched in _M_<sub>1</sub> to a neighbor preferred to the other. Thus, \(\mathsf{wt}_{M_1}(a,b)\le 0\) and so \(\beta_a+\beta_b=0\ge\mathsf{wt}_{M_1}(a,b)\).

This finishes the proof of the claim. \(\sqcap\sqcup\)
