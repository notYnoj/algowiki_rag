For the branch \(\mu=0\), the Taylor expansion with respect to \(k\Delta x\) is tedious but straightforward. The detailed derivation of the final result (42) is omitted here. We only claim that the requirement \(\gamma_2(k\Delta x)^2\checkmark 1\), stated above (42), is needed in the following equation:

\(\frac{1-[\mathcal{G}_0^{(0)}(k)]^{\gamma_2}}{1-\mathcal{G}_0^{(0)}(k)}=\gamma_2+O((k\Delta x)^2),\) (C.47)

which expands the coefficient in (C.46).
