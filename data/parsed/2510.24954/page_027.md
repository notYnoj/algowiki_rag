## A Lower Bounds for \(m^{1-\epsilon}\)-Sized Steiner Shortcuts

In this section, we present a simple lower bound ruling out the existence of strongly sublinear Steiner reachability shortcuts with subpolynomial diameter.

**Theorem A.1.** _For every sufficiently small constant_ \(\epsilon>0\)_, there exists an infinite family of n-node, m-edge directed graphs G, where_ \(m\in\left[n,n^{2-4\epsilon^{1/2}+\epsilon}\right]\)_, such that every Steiner reachability shortcut of G has size at least_ \(\tilde{\Omega}(m^{1-\epsilon})\) _or diameter at least_ \(\Omega(n^\epsilon)\).
