2. Recall Definition 2.5 of partially integer vertices: these are (_X,Z,D_) vertices whose _D_ coordinates are integer. In Example 2.7 we present such a vertex, yet when projected to _D_-space it is no longer a vertex. Are there partially integer vertices that remain vertices under the projection? Property 2 does not rule them out. It would be interesting to find out either way.
