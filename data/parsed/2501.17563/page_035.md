6: Summary of all tree topologies up to \(n\le 8\) nodes. Following, _italicized_ refer to column titles. _Frac_, _Vs_ and _denoms_. are short for fractional, vertices and denominators. _Topology U_<sub>(_n,i_)</sub> correspond to Figure 6. All columns are with respect to _D_-space except for the last. Out of all _Primary Directions_ (Definition 2.4) only few determine _False Facets_, which reveal _Frac Vs_. These vertices all happen to be half-integer (_D-space denom._ of 1 and 2). _Frac Vs Classes_ is the number of equivalence classes of non-STT vertices by automorphic symmetries. _XZD-space denom._ lists denominators of coordinates in vertices of the LP polytope. We were able to enumerate all the vertices for topologies marked with ∗. For the rest, we sampled random vertices by solving the LP in random _XZD_-directions. Lists without ∗ may be incomplete: For example, every extension of _U_<sub>(5,0)</sub> should have third-integer vertices by Theorem 3.12, yet _U_<sub>(7,3)</sub> (allegedly) does not.
