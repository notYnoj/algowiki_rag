This example shows that the container process depends not only on vert(_I_), but also on the colors assigned to its vertices; moreover, even specifying the colors selected in the first few iterations does not determine a unique projected container vert(_C<sub>t</sub>_), since the two processes have the same selected-color sequence but different pivot vertices and different projected containers.
