A large gap remains between the _O(n_<sup>6</sup>)-time upper bound and the \(O(n^{2-\epsilon})\)-time lower bound for CT-LCS in the general case, and how to close this gap is an intriguing open question. Another open question is to close the gaps in the alphabet sizes for the subquadratic time complexities of CT-MSeq and CT-LCS.
