with \(\Delta_{ee}^M\equiv\Delta\lambda_{31}^ML/2\). Here, following the DMP convention \[47, 72\], the matter-dressed splittings carrying a superscript _M_ (such as \(\Delta\lambda_{31}^M\) of Section 9.1) are quoted as energies, i.e. already divided by 2_E_ relative to the bare Cardano eigenvalues \(\lambda_i\) of \(\Lambda=2EH_\mathrm{f}\); correspondingly \(\Delta\tilde{m}_{ee}^2=\Delta m_{ee}^2\sqrt{(\mathrm{cos}2\theta_{13}-\hat{A})^2+\mathrm{sin}^22\theta_{13}}\). For reactor baselines \(L\lesssim 60\)km (JUNO, Daya Bay), \(\hat{A}\checkmark 1\) and matter corrections are negligible at the O(0.3%) level \[74\]; _P<sub>ee</sub>_ is independent of \(\delta_\mathrm{CP}\) \[60\], making reactor \(\theta_{13}\) a clean observable. From unitarity, \(P_{\mu\tau}=1-P_{\mu\mu}-P_{\mu e}\).

### 7.4 Domain of Validity and Perturbative Divergence

As \(\hat{A}\rightarrow 1\), \(T_\mathrm{atm}\propto(1-\hat{A})^{-2}\) and \(T_\mathrm{int}\propto(1-\hat{A})^{-1}\) diverge--a failure of the expansion near degenerate eigenvalues, at

\(E_{\hat{A}=1}=\frac{\Delta m_{31}^2}{2V_\mathrm{CC}}\approx 13\mathrm{GeV}(\rho=2.6\mathrm{gc}\mathrm{m}^{-3}).\) (41)

This lies well above the long-baseline beam peaks and is treated by resummation in Section 10. (The energy scales inversely with density: the DUNE benchmark of  2 uses \(\rho=2.84\mathrm{gc}\mathrm{m}^{-3}\), which lowers \(E_{\hat{A}=1}\) to about 12GeV.)

## 8 Wave-Packet Decoherence Corrections

### 8.1 Framework and Coherence Lengths

Physical neutrinos are produced and detected as wave-packets of finite spatial extent \[35, 36, 37\]. Different mass eigenstates propagate with different group velocities and separate after the coherence length \[39, 40, 41\]. A Gaussian model \[38, 42\] assigns a production position uncertainty \(\sigma_x\sim 10^{-15}\)-10<sup>-13</sup>m. In constant-density matter the coherence length for the pair (_i,j_) is \[39\]

\(L_\mathrm{coh}^{ij}=\frac{4\sqrt{2}E^2\sigma_x}{|\Delta\lambda_{ij}|},\) (42)

where \(\Delta\lambda_{ij}\) is the matter-dressed mass-squared splitting (the eigenvalue difference of \(\Lambda=2EH_\mathrm{f}\)); in vacuum \(\Delta\lambda_{ij}\rightarrow\Delta m_{ij}^2\) and this reduces to the familiar form \(L_\mathrm{coh}^{ij}=4\sqrt{2}E^2\sigma_x/|\Delta m_{ij}^2|\). For the 31-pair at _E_=0.6GeV, with \(\sigma_x\sim 10^{-13}\)m and \(\Delta m_{31}^2\approx 2.5\times 10^{-3}\mathrm{eV}^2\),

\(L_\mathrm{coh}^{31}=\frac{4\sqrt{2}\left(0.6\mathrm{GeV}\right)^2\left(10^{-13}\mathrm{m}\right)}{2.5\times 10^{-3}\mathrm{eV}^2}\approx 8\times 10^4\mathrm{km},\) (43)

two to three orders of magnitude larger than the T2K/HK baseline. Decoherence is therefore negligible for terrestrial long-baseline experiments, but only by a factor \(L/L_\mathrm{coh}^{31}\sim 10^{-3}\)-10<sup>-2</sup> ( 5)--not the much larger margin sometimes quoted. Solar neutrinos are the exception: \(L/L_\mathrm{coh}\gg 1\), coherences wash out, and the survival probability reduces to the incoherent sum \(P_{ee}^\mathrm{solar}=\sum_i|U_{ei}|^2|V_{eei}|^2\) in the adiabatic MSW picture \[19, 59\].
