### 8.2 Wave-Packet Modified Probabilities

The Gaussian model multiplies each interference term by the decoherence factor \(D_{ij}(L)=\mathrm{exp}[-L^2/2(L_\mathrm{coh}^{ij})^2]\), giving

\(P_{\alpha\beta}^{WP}(L)=\delta_{\alpha\beta}-4\sum_{i>j}\mathrm{Re}[V_{e\beta i}V_{e\alpha i}^*V_{e\beta j}^*V_{e\alpha j}]\mathrm{sin}^2\frac{\Delta\lambda_{ij}L}{4E}D_{ij}(L)\)<br>
\(+2\sum_{i>j}\mathrm{Im}[V_{e\beta i}V_{e\alpha i}^*V_{e\beta j}^*V_{e\alpha j}]\mathrm{sin}\frac{\Delta\lambda_{ij}L}{2E}D_{ij}(L).\) (44)

## 9 MSW Resonance and the Sub-Resonant Regime

### 9.1 Resonance Condition

The MSW resonance, \(\mathrm{sin}^22\theta_{13}^M=1\), occurs at

\(E_\mathrm{res}=\frac{\Delta m_{31}^2\mathrm{cos}2\theta_{13}}{2\sqrt{2}}G_FN_e\approx 12\mathrm{GeV}(\rho=2.6\mathrm{gc}\mathrm{m}^{-3}),\) (45)

consistent with the standard crustal estimate (the resonance moves to ∼5-7GeV at mantle densities \(\rho\sim 4.5\)-5.5gcm<sup>-3</sup>, scaling as \(1/\rho\)). The matter-dressed splitting \(\Delta\lambda_{31}^M=(\Delta m_{31}^2/2E)\sqrt{(\mathrm{cos}2\theta_{13}-\hat{A})^2+\mathrm{sin}^22\theta_{13}}\) attains its minimum \((\Delta m_{31}^2/2E)\mathrm{sin}2\theta_{13}\) at res onance, a level-avoided crossing; the full width in \(\hat{A}\) at half-maximum mixing is \(\delta\hat{A}=2\mathrm{sin}2\theta_{13}\approx 0.59\).

#### T2K/HK sub-resonant regime.

At \(\rho=2.6\mathrm{gc}\mathrm{m}^{-3}\), \(\hat{A}\approx 0.078(E/\mathrm{GeV})\). The T2K and HK beams peak at 0.6GeV over 295km, placing them deep in the sub-resonant region, \(\hat{A}|_{0.6\mathrm{GeV}}\approx 0.05\). Matter effects modify probabilities at the few-percent level, with no level-avoided crossing and no proximity to the eigenvalue degeneracy (\(\hat{A}\rightarrow 1\)) that drives the perturbative divergence of Section 7.4. The paper makes no claim of an MSW resonance for T2K/HK.

### 9.2 Varying Density: Adiabaticity and Landau-Zener

For strictly constant density, \(d\rho/dx=0\) implies adiabaticity parameter \(\gamma\rightarrow\infty\) and purely oscillatory evolution. For varying-density extensions, a neutrino crossing an MSW resonance has non-adiabatic transition probability \(P_c=\mathrm{exp}(-\frac{\pi}{2}\gamma_\mathrm{res})\) \[61, 62\]; for solar neutrinos \(\gamma_\mathrm{res}\sim 10^3\), so \(P_c\rightarrow 0\) and the conversion is adiabatic.
