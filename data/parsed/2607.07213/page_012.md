### 6.3 Bloch Vector Representation

Any \(3\times 3\) Hermitian unit-trace matrix admits \(\varrho=\frac{1}{3}\mathbf{1}+\sum_ar_a\lambda_a/2\) with \(r_a=\mathrm{Tr}(\varrho\lambda_a)\), and the von Neumann equation becomes the precession equation

\(\frac{dr_a}{dx}=\sum_{b,c}f_{abc}h_br_c,\) (33)

where _f<sub>abc</sub>_ are the su(3) structure constants.

## 7 Perturbative Expansion in Matter

### 7.1 Expansion Variables

We use the atmospheric phase, the matter ratio, and the \(\delta_\mathrm{CP}\)-independent Jarlskog combination,

\(\Delta\equiv\frac{\Delta m_{31}^2L}{4E},\hat{A}\equiv\frac{a}{\Delta m_{31}^2},J_\mathrm{CP}\equiv s_{12}c_{12}s_{23}c_{23}s_{13}c_{13}^2.\) (34)

Here \(\Delta\) and \(\hat{A}\) are auxiliary variables absorbing recurrent combinations, while _J_<sub>CP</sub> is a fixed combination of mixing parameters \[55\].

### 7.2 Appearance Probability \(\nu_\mu\rightarrow\nu_e\)

Following \[45, 46\],

\(P_{\mu e}\approx T_\mathrm{atm}+T_\mathrm{int}+T_\mathrm{sol},\) (35)

\(T_\mathrm{atm}=4s_{23}^2s_{13}^2c_{13}^2\frac{\mathrm{sin}^2[(1-\hat{A})\Delta]}{(1-\hat{A})^2},\) (36)

\(T_\mathrm{int}=8\alpha J_\mathrm{CP}\mathrm{cos}(\Delta+\delta_\mathrm{CP})\frac{\mathrm{sin}(\hat{A}\Delta)}{\hat{A}}\frac{\mathrm{sin}[(1-\hat{A})\Delta]}{1-\hat{A}},\) (37)

\(T_\mathrm{sol}=4\alpha^2c_{13}^2c_{23}^2s_{12}^2c_{12}^2\frac{\mathrm{sin}^2(\hat{A}\Delta)}{\hat{A}^2}.\) (38)

_T_<sub>atm</sub> is leading; _T_<sub>int</sub> is the \(\mathcal{O}(\alpha)\) interference term carrying the CP-phase dependence; _T_<sub>sol</sub> is the \(\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)\) solar term.
