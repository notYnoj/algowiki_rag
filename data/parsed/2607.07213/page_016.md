## 11 CP Violation: Analytic Structure in Matter

### 11.1 CP Asymmetry and Its Decomposition

The appearance-channel CP asymmetry is \(\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}\equiv P(\nu_\mu\rightarrow\nu_e)-P(\bar{\nu}_\mu\rightarrow\bar{\nu}_e)\). Using \(\mathrm{Im}[V_{e\beta i}V_{e\alpha i}^*V_{e\beta j}^*V_{e\alpha j}]=+J_\mathrm{mat}\) for \((i,j)\in\{(2,1),(3,2),(1,3)\}\) and -_J_<sub>mat</sub> otherwise, together with a trigonometric product identity,

\(\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}^\mathrm{genuine}=-16J_\mathrm{mat}\mathrm{sin}\frac{\Delta\lambda_{21}L}{4E}\mathrm{sin}\frac{\Delta\lambda_{31}L}{4E}\mathrm{sin}\frac{\Delta\lambda_{32}L}{4E}.\) (47)

### 11.2 Genuine vs. Fake CP Violation

The total asymmetry splits as \[57, 58\] \(\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}^\mathrm{total}=\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}^\mathrm{genuine}+\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}^\mathrm{fake}\), where the genuine term is odd under \(J\rightarrow-J\) and vanishes at _J_=0, while

\(\mathcal{A}_\mathrm{CP}^\mathrm{fake}\approx 4s_{23}^2s_{13}^2c_{13}^2\frac{\mathrm{sin}^2[(1-\hat{A})\Delta]}{(1-\hat{A})^2}-4s_{23}^2s_{13}^2c_{13}^2\frac{\mathrm{sin}^2[(1+\hat{A})\Delta]}{(1+\hat{A})^2}\) (48)

is odd in \(\hat{A}\) and survives even at \(\delta_\mathrm{CP}=0\). For strictly uniform matter, a non-zero T-asymmetry \(\mathcal{A}_T\equiv P(\nu_\mu\rightarrow\nu_e)-P(\nu_e\rightarrow\nu_\mu)\) is a clean signature of fundamental T-violation \[63\], free from the fake asymmetry.

## 12 Magic Baseline

Both _T_<sub>int</sub> and _T_<sub>sol</sub> vanish when \(\mathrm{sin}(\hat{A}\Delta)=0\), i.e. \(V_\mathrm{CC}L/2=n\pi\), which is energy- independent. The _n_=1 baseline is the magic baseline \[60\]

\(L_\mathrm{magic}=\frac{2\pi}{V_\mathrm{CC}}=\frac{2\pi m_N}{\sqrt{2}G_F\rho Y_e}\approx\frac{1.62\times 10^4}{Y_e(\rho/\mathrm{gcm}^{-3})}\mathrm{km},\) (49)

where \(P_{\mu e}\rightarrow T_\mathrm{atm}\) is independent of \(\delta_\mathrm{CP}\) and \(\Delta m_{21}^2\). At _Y<sub>e</sub>_=0.5 and a representative crossing density \(\rho\approx 3.0\mathrm{gcm}^{-3}\) this gives \(L_\mathrm{magic}\approx 1.08\times 10^4\mathrm{km}\); for the deeper mantle density (\(\rho\approx 4.3\mathrm{gcm}^{-3}\)) sampled by a chord of this length, _L_<sub>magic</sub> drops to the often- quoted \(\approx 7.6\times 10^3\mathrm{km}\). Either way the DUNE baseline lies far from the magic condition: at _L_=1300km, _E_=2.5GeV, \(\hat{A}\Delta\approx 0.11\pi\), so _T_<sub>int</sub> and _T_<sub>sol</sub> remain sizable ( 7).
