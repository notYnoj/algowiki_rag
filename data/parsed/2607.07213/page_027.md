Together with Eqs. (51)-(55) these give all six closed-form components of \(J=\partial P_{\mu e}/\partial p\). Each is a smooth analytic function of the parameters, so the Jacobian is continuous everywhere except where a structure-factor denominator \((1-\hat{A})\) or \(\hat{A}\) vanishes; those points are handled by the removable-singularity limits of Section 14. The disappearance derivatives \(\partial P_{\mu\mu}/\partial p_k\) follow identically from Eq. (39).
