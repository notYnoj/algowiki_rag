## B The Jarlskog Invariant

The Jarlskog invariant is the rephasing-invariant measure of CP violation in three-generation mixing \[55\]: \(\text{Im}(U_{\alpha i}U_{\alpha j}^*U_{\beta i}^*U_{\beta j})=J\sum_{\gamma,k}\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\epsilon_{ijk}\) for \(\alpha\neq\beta\), \(i\neq j\). In the PDG parameterization \(J=s_{12}c_{12}s_{23}c_{23}s_{13}c_{13}^2\mathrm{sin}\delta_\mathrm{CP}=J_\mathrm{CP}\mathrm{sin}\delta_\mathrm{CP}\); with NuFIT 6.0 values, _J_<sub>CP</sub>≈0.0335 and, with \(\delta_\mathrm{CP}\approx 212^\circ\), \(|J|=|J_\mathrm{CP}\mathrm{sin}\delta_\mathrm{CP}|\approx 0.018\). In matter, \(J\rightarrow J_\mathrm{mat}\) via the Naumov- Harrison-Scott identity (Eq. 25).

## C Gell-Mann Decomposition of the Hamiltonian

The diagonal Gell-Mann components of _H_<sub>f</sub> are

\(h_3=\mathrm{Tr}(H_\mathrm{f}\lambda_3)=H_{f,11}-H_{f,22},\) (59)

\(h_8=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\big(H_{f,11}+H_{f,22}-2H_{f,33}\big),\) (60)

with _h_<sub>1</sub>_,...,h_<sub>7</sub> following analogously from \(h_a=\mathrm{Tr}(H_\mathrm{f}\lambda_a)\). The off-diagonal components encode flavor transition amplitudes; _h_<sub>3</sub> and _h_<sub>8</sub> encode the diagonal energy differences, and |**_h_**| sets the Bloch-vector precession frequency (Eq. 33 via Section 6.3).
