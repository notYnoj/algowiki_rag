<tbc>Let us condition on the teleportation measurement result being \(\kappa_1\). Then, after applying the correction \(P_{\kappa_1}^\top\) on Z(2), where we use the fact that \(P_{\kappa_1}^\top\overline{P_{\kappa_1}}=\mathbb{1}\), and tracing out the registers X(1)Y(1) that were measured for the teleportation, we get

\(\sigma^{(1)}=\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i|u_i^{(1)}\rangle\langle u_i^{(1)}|_{\mathsf{Z}(2)\mathsf{X}(2)\mathsf{X}(3)\dots\mathsf{X}(n-1)\mathsf{E}}\otimes\otimes_{p=2}^{n-1}|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|_{\mathsf{Y}(p)\mathsf{Z}(p+1)}.\)

Therefore, the result of teleportation on the registers X(1)Y(1)Z(2) of \(\sigma\) is a relabelling of X(1) to Z(2).

Repeating the same argument for X(_p_)Y(_p_)Z(_p_+1) with \(p\in\{2,\dots,n-1\}\) and conditioning on the measurement outcomes \((\kappa_2,\dots,\kappa_{n-1})\) leads to

\(\sigma^{(n-1)}=\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i|u_i^{(n-1)}\rangle\langle u_i^{(n-1)}|_{\mathsf{Z}(2)\mathsf{Z}(3)\cdots\mathsf{Z}(n)\mathsf{E}},\)

where \(|u_i^{(n-1)}\rangle\) is obtained from |_u<sub>i</sub>_〉 by relabelling X(_p_) as Z(_p_+1) for every \(p\in[n-1]\).

Hence, the resulting state on \(\mathsf{Z}(2)\mathsf{Z}(3)\cdots\mathsf{Z}(n)\mathsf{E}\) is equal to the initial state on \(\mathsf{X}(1)\mathsf{X}(2)\cdots\mathsf{X}(n-1)\mathsf{E}\) up to the relabelling.
