Chapter 4 focuses on the details of the absorbing strategy and also includes the proof of a result associated with the work of Chapters 3 and 4 which does not directly relate to the proof of Theorem 1.2, but is key to the proof of Theorem 1.3. Chapter 5 concerns the random greedy counting process, and some parity modifications that take us to a subgraph \(H\subseteq\mathcal{T}\) on which it remains for us to run the iterative matching process to obtain a small leave _L_<sup>∗</sup> which can be absorbed by _A_<sup>∗</sup> when the necessary divisibility conditions are satisfied. Chapter 6 concerns the details of this iterative matching process, in which we shift to considering _weighted_ subhypergraphs of T and build the matching covering all remaining vertices but some ‘good’ leave _L_<sup>∗</sup> over _O_(log(_n_)) such hypergraphs. Finally, in Chapter 7, we give the remaining details for the proof of Theorem 1.3 and make some concluding remarks.
