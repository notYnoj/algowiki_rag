## C. The filtered term _h_: recall is the truncated frontier mass, with \(\beta=1\)

We sweep local density \(\sigma_\mathrm{loc}\) (diffuse random-balanced labels and spatially-clustered labels at matched _global_ selectivity) against search width _L_, measuring post-filter recall@10 with per-label GT on one label-blind base graph ( II).

_a) (1) The collapse variable is_ \(x=\sigma_\mathrm{loc}L/k\)_, and it is tight.:_ Cells with equal \(\sigma_\mathrm{loc}L\) but different (\(\sigma_\mathrm{loc},L\)) agree closely (e.g. four independent pairs at _x_=1.26 give 95.42/95.27/95.29/95.40), and the collapse is scale-invariant \(10\mathrm{K}\leftrightarrow 100\mathrm{K}\). Local, not global, selectivity governs recall. The clustered slice at global \(\sigma=0.042\) (which _global_ density would place at ≈31) recalls 73.5 because its measured \(\hat{\sigma}_\mathrm{loc}=0.168\); \(\sigma_\mathrm{loc}\) over-predicts it by a comparable margin (finding 3), so it is the better of two imperfect predictors rather than a sufficient statistic.

_b) (2)_ \(\beta=1\) _is structural, and the law then has no free parameter.:_ The measurement post-filters the search’s returned candidate list, of length exactly _L_, and takes the top-_k_ of the survivors; a frontier of 1.66_L_ candidates cannot be filtered out of a list of _L_, so \(\beta=1\) is forced by the pipeline rather than fitted (Assumption 4). The data agrees independently: in the unsaturated regime measured recall equals \(\sigma_\mathrm{loc}L/k\) with unit slope (at _x_=0.078,0.156,0.313,0.629 the ratio recall_/x_ is 1.00,1.00,1.00,0.98), which is the exact small-_x_ limit of Lemma 7(ii) only when \(\beta=1\).
