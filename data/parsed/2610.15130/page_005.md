In sum, three of Theorem 8’s four parts hold unconditionally (the additive assembly by subadditivity, the derived parameter- free filtered term, and the named measurable ANN deficit), while the staleness term is a _calibrated scaling law_ (its prefactor and exponents fit on one substrate, dataset family, and _c/N_ range). The theorem is thus a proved decomposition of which one component is empirical: weaker than a distribution- free \((\epsilon,\delta)\) guarantee, stronger than a recall estimator that predicts without constraining. A first-principles derivation of the degradation exponent would replace the calibration with a constant; we state it as open.

## IV. SYSTEM: CACHE (C2) AND SCHEDULER (C3)

The two engineering contributions instantiate the theorem. C2 (the staging cache) absorbs within-cycle staleness on the query path so the loss the theorem bounds is the disk-graph term, not cache coverage; C3 (the scheduler) re-prioritizes background repair without ever re-sizing a merge, which is the only lever anti-amortization leaves. We prototype both on a FreshDiskANN fork with an `io\_uring` backend. _All numbers below are measured on this prototype unless marked otherwise._

### A. Substrate: one label-blind Yamana

The disk index is a _single_ Yamana graph over the label- agnostic vector set; predicates and tenant labels are query- time post-filter tags and never enter graph construction or the merge operator M_<sub>c</sub>_.
