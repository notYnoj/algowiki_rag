<tbc>Since \(\|1-Q\|,\|P\|\le 1\), compression preserves the bound from Theorem 6.4, and the reverse triangle inequality gives the claim. □

For wider spectra, shorter midpoint steps keep length times width below \(2\pi\), and Proposition 6.6 bounds the comparison for such unequal pieces by piece integrals and frame jumps.

**Proposition 6.6.** _Let_ \(a_0<\cdots<a_n\)_, and let K be a continuous Hamiltonian on_ \[_a_<sub>0</sub>_,a<sub>n</sub>\], with evolution U, U(a_<sub>0</sub>)=1_. _Proof._ On piece _j_, the chain rule with \(t=\xi_j+\lambda_j\tau\) gives the rescaled generator \(\lambda_jK(\xi_j+\lambda_j\tau)\) and constant generator _h<sub>j</sub>_. ## 6.2. Adiabatic consequences at unit steps.

We apply Theorem 6.5 to Jansen-Ruskai-Seiler, then to quantum linear systems along An and Lin’s adiabatic quantum computing schedule AQC(3/2).

**Corollary 6.7.** _Assume the continuous Jansen-Ruskai-Seiler theorem_ \[16, Theorem 3\]_. Fix an integer_ \(T\ge 1\)_. Let H be C_<sup>2</sup> _on an open interval containing_ \[0,1\]_, with_ \(\|H'\|\le L\) _and_ \(\|H''\|\le 2\nu\)_, and let H(j/T_) _be_ \(\gamma\)_-separated of width at most_ \(\bar{w}\) _for_ 0_\<j\<T. \(\|(1-Q)W_TP\|\le\frac{J_m(\|H'\|,\|H''\|,g)+c_0L+c_1L^2+c_2\nu}{T},\)

_with the Jansen-Ruskai-Seiler coefficient J<sub>m</sub> of Definition 3.14 and c<sub>i</sub> from Definition 6.3._

_Proof._ Jansen-Ruskai-Seiler \[16, Theorem 3\] bounds \(\|(1-Q)U(T)P\|\) by \(J_m(\|H'\|,\|H''\|,g)/T\). Integrating \(\|H''\|\le 2\nu\) gives the Lipschitz bound on \(H'\) required by Theorem 6.5, which adds \((c_0L+c_1L^2+c_2\nu)/T\). □
