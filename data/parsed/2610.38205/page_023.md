Combining Lemmas 4.5, 4.7 and 4.8 yields the following corollary.

**Corollary 4.9.** _For every constant_ \(\varepsilon>0\)_, there does not exist an_ \(O(n^{2.5-\varepsilon})\) _algorithm for_ \(\alpha\)_- navigability testing unless the APSP conjecture is falsified._
