PE mapping vectorizes the inner update across a PU’s sixteen PE lanes: a row activation yields an 8192-bit block and an APSP tile row is \(256\times 32\) bits, so each PE takes a 512-bit slice matching the row-buffer width. Instruction selection emits native operations from the G1 semi-ring, so the operator pair selects which Max/Min sub-units run (an add with min/compare for (min, +), a max-logic sequence for (max, +)), not a fixed opcode sequence. One back-end thus serves both workloads.

Command-stream emission is target-specific, producing a _memory layout_ (each object’s tier, channel, and bank-group from G3) and an _execution schedule_ (per-PU command order and communication events from G2/G4). Per PU it interleaves memory commands (ACTIVATE, reads over the 1024-bit hybrid-bond interface, precharge), datapath commands, and G4 messages (broadcast/ barrier for APSP, queue send/recv for alignment), subject to two constraints: a row stays open across all its reads before precharge, and the schedule honors the Floyd-Warshall phase barriers and the queue depth bounding the pipeline.

### 3.5 Scope of the Abstraction

A recurrence is admitted by the DP dialect when (a) \(\oplus\) is associative and commutative and \(\otimes\) distributes over it, so tiles may be reduced in any order; (b) the dependence pattern is affine in the loop indices, so tiling is legal; and (c) the cell update is data-independent in its _control flow_, so a static schedule exists.  1 lists problems meeting these conditions; all reuse the same passes, differing only in the operator pair the front-end records and the traversal G2 selects.

Three classes fall outside. Data-dependent control flow (X-drop or adaptive-band alignment, where the band shifts with the running score) violates (c), requiring a speculative or dynamically re-tiled schedule that G2 does not synthesize. Non-grid dependence graphs, such as matrix-chain parenthesization or tree DP, violate<tbc>

<tbc>(b). Non-associative accumulations (e.g. fixed-point probabilistic scoring, where reduction order changes the result) violate (a). The most valuable next step is to relax (c) by compiling adaptive-band alignment, which GenDRAM’s hardware already supports.

## 4 Evaluation

### 4.1 Methodology

**Platform.** We evaluate on the GenDRAM cycle-accurate simulator \[15\], configured as its authors specify: a 32 GB, 1024-layer M3D stack in 8 tiers of 4 GB, _t_<sub>RCD</sub> from 2.29 to 22.88 ns, 16 channels, and 32 PUs (8 Search / 24 Compute) at 1 GHz. Compilation itself runs on AMD EPYC nodes (256 cores, 768 GB) under SLURM.

**Workloads.** For APSP we use dense instances of _ca-GrQc_ (5,242 nodes) and _p2p-Gnutella08_ (6,301 nodes) \[13\], plus a 65,536-node road network from _OpenStreetMap_ \[16\]. For genomics we align long reads of 2-10 kbp, generated by PBSIM \[19\], against GRCh38 \[17\] under two error models: PacBio (15%) and ONT 2D (30%). These are the datasets used to evaluate the GenDRAM hardware \[15\].
