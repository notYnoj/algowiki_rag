## 5 Conclusion

By augmenting previous algorithms for computing lower bounds with run-time optimizations, parallelization, and a recursive memory I/O scheme to allow sequential reading and writing once values become to large to store in RAM, we established new best lower bounds on the Chvátal-Sankoff constants in the classical two binary string case as well as in the extended, general cases. All code is open source and available at `https://github. com/Statistics-of-Subsequences/PaperMaterials`.

While approximations of the value of the Chvátal- Sankoff constant for pairs of binary strings have been made (see  1), it seems that most of the work for the general case has been focused on tightening the upper and lower bounds. We believe that performing calculations to obtain precise approximations of different \(\gamma_{\sigma,d}\) could be an avenue for future research yielding noteworthy results. Kiwi and Soto, as well as Steele, express interest in finding a relationship between different \(\gamma_{\sigma,d}\) [16, 25]. We agree that such a relationship, if it exists, would be interesting, and also wonder if having precise approximations for the constants would aid in finding a relationship. We also believe that optimizing algorithms which generate upper bounds on the Chvátal- Sankoff constants may be an attractive direction for future work.
