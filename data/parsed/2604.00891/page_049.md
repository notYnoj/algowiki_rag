We next show that for a planar multigraph _H_=(_U,F_), the number of non-isomorphic separations of size \(t\in\tilde{\mathcal{O}}(\sqrt{|U|})\) is in \(2^{\mathcal{O}(\psi(H)/\mathrm{log}(\psi(H)))}\). **Lemma 17.** _Given a planar multigraph H_=(_U,F_) _and a non-negative integer_ \(t\in\mathcal{O}(\psi(H)^\frac{2}{3})\)_, the number_ \(\sigma_t(H)\) _of non-isomorphic separations of size at most t of H is in_ \(2^{\mathcal{O}(\psi(H)/\mathrm{log}(\psi(H)))}\)_. The separations can be enumerated in_ \(\mathcal{O}(\psi(H)^3\sigma_t(H))\) _time._

_Proof._ We follow the proof strategy for simple planar graphs by Bodlaender, Nederlof, and van der Zanden \[BNvdZ16\] to count the number of non-isomorphic separations (_X,Y_) of _H_ of size at most _t_. There are \(\genfrac(){pt}{2}{|U|}{t}\le\psi(H)^t\in\psi(H)^{\mathcal{O}(\psi(H)^{2/3})}\) possible choices for the separator \(Z=X\cap Y\) and all subsets of _U_ of size at most _t_ can be enumerated in \(\mathcal{O}(\genfrac(){pt}{2}{|U|}{t})\subseteq\mathcal{O}(\sigma_t(H))\) time as \(\sigma_t(H)\ge\genfrac(){pt}{2}{|U|}{t}\). Next, we compute the connected components of _H_\[_U_\\_Z_\] in \(\mathcal{O}(|U|+|F|)\subseteq\mathcal{O}(\psi(H))\) time. Let the **_weight_** of a connected component _C_ in _H_\[_U_\\_S_\] denote the number of edges with at least one endpoint in _C_, that is, the number of edges between two vertices in _C_ plus the number of edges between vertices in _C_ and vertices in _Z_. A connected component is **_heavy_** if its weight is at least \(\mathrm{log}(\psi(H))/180\) and it is **_light_** otherwise.

Next, we show how to construct a (simple, bipartite, and planar) graph \(H'\) with _Z_ as the vertices on one side of the bipartition and a new vertex set _B_ on the other side. We start with the graph _H_, contract all connected components in _H_\[_U_\\_Z_\] into single vertices (the vertices in _B_), remove all self loops and parallel edges, and remove all edges between two vertices in _Z_. Note that the above can be computed in \(\mathcal{O}(\psi(H))\) time. It is known that there are at most \(5|Z|\le 5t\) vertices in _B_ of degree at least 6. Moreover, there are at most \(37|Z|\le 37t\) subsets _S_ of _Z_ such that there exists a vertex in _B_ whose neighborhood is exactly _S_ \[GHO<sup>+</sup>13\]. We say a connected component of _H_\[_U_\\_Z_\] is **_highly connected_** if the corresponding vertex in \(H'\) has at least six neighbors in _Z_. Connected components that are light and _not_ highly connected are **_small_**.
