The difficulty we have encountered is that in an approximate toroidal configuration nearly all toroidal diagonals are occupied. This makes absorbers like those in our proof difficult to find.<tbc>

• The list of conjectures at the end of [1] contains several generalizations of the _n_-queens problem, for example to boards with different topologies and higher dimensions. It would be interesting if these problems could be attacked with techniques similar to the one in this paper.
