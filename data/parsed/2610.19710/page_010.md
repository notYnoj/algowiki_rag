**Figure 2** Illustration of the update procedure after an edge insertion on a grid graph. The numbers in the cells indicate the potential \(\phi\), kept as internal state, while the cell color represents the coloring obtained from \(mod(\phi,3)\). In this example, the two components that are being connected are not parity-consistent. Not shown: if step (d) reaches potential 0 without filling the entire edit area, then the remaining area is filled with alternating values 0 and 1 to complete the coloring. Note also that our algorithm works on any bipartite graph, not only grids.
