While the upper bound follows from Theorem 1, the lower bound was given in [11], where the authors proved that \(\Omega(logn)\) rounds are necessary to 3-color a 2-dimensional grid graph in the online-LOCAL model. Since it is a stronger model than the dynamic-LOCAL model, the same lower bound applies to the dynamic-LOCAL model as well. Furthermore, Corollary 2 holds even if the graph is equipped with oriented edges, which is an extra information that can only make the problem easier, but it was proven in [8] that an algorithm cannot take advantage of this information to solve the problem faster than \(\Omega(logn)\) rounds in the online-LOCAL model.
