## B Basic Algebra Facts and Inequalities

**Fact B.1.** _Let_ \(0\le x\le y\le a\)_. Then we can show_<tbc>

\(\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{1}{(y-x)^2}\ge\frac{2}{a^2}\)

<tbc>_Proof._ Since \(0<y-x\le a-x\), then \((y-x)^2\le(a-x)^2\). This implies that

\(\frac{1}{(y-x)^2}\ge\frac{1}{(a-x)^2}\) (19)

Let \(f(x):=\frac{1}{x^2}\). Note that _f(x_) is a convex function in [0_,a_], thus

\(f(x)+f(a-x)\ge 2f(a-x+x)=\frac{2}{a^2}\) (20)

Thus, we have

\(\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{1}{(y-x)^2}\ge\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{1}{(a-x)^2}\)<br>
\(\ge\frac{2}{a^2}\)

where the first step follows from Eq. (19), and the second step follows from Eq. (20).

Thus, we complete the proof.
