2. Can we establish a conditional lower bound of _n_<sup>2</sup>_m_<sup>1-_o_(1)</sup> for all _m_, against combinatorial algorithms? Such a lower bound is not known even for directed graphs with general edge capacities.<tbc>

3. Can we design an \(\hat{O}(m^2)\) time combinatorial algorithm for directed graphs with unit edge- capacities? Our adaptation of the framework in [AKT20a] may hint its robustness.<tbc>

4. Finally, the most significant open question after this work is: Can we design a subquartic nondeterministic algorithm for directed graphs with edge-capacities? Since our proof for the cut upper bounds in Lemma 2.2 holds for this setting too, it is enough to develop a fast nondeterministic algorithm that proves flow lower bounds.
