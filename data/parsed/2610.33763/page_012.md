• {_A<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub>_: Sequence of matrices indexed by size/discretization parameter _n_.<tbc>

• _T<sub>n</sub>(f_): Toeplitz matrix of size _n_ generated by symbol _f_.<tbc>

• _C<sub>n</sub>(f_): Circulant matrix of size _n_ generated by symbol _f_.<tbc>

• _H<sub>n</sub>(f_): Hankel matrix of size _n_ generated by symbol _f_.<tbc>

• Symbol (\(\kappa\)): Measurable function associated with a matrix-sequence, e.g., \(\{A_n\}_n\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\kappa\).<tbc>

• Symbol (\(\xi\)): Measurable function associated with a matrix-sequence, e.g., \(\{B_n\}_n\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\xi\).<tbc>

• \(D=[0,1]^d\times[-\pi,\pi]^d\): Typical domain for the symbol of a _d_-level GLT sequence.<tbc>

• \(\sim_\sigma\): Singular value distribution.<tbc>

• \(\sim_\lambda\): Eigenvalue distribution.<tbc>

• ∼<sub>GLT</sub>: Matrix-sequence is a GLT sequence with symbol.<tbc>

• ran(_M_): The column space (range) of a matrix _M_.<tbc>

• essRan(_g_): the essential range of a measurable function \(g:D\subseteq\mathbb{R}^t\rightarrow Y\) , where _Y_ is C, C_<sup>r</sup>_, \(\mathbb{C}^{r\times r}\), or the Grassmannian of subspaces of C_<sup>r</sup>_, i.e.<tbc>

<tbc>\(\mathrm{essRan}(g)=\big\{y\in Y:\mu_t\big(\{x\in D:g(x)\in B_Y(y,\epsilon)\}\big)>0\forall\epsilon>0\big\},\)<tbc>

<tbc>where \(\mu_t\) is the Lebesgue measure on _D_ and \(B_Y(y,\epsilon)\) is the ball of radius \(\epsilon\) centered at _y_ in _Y_ (with respect to the natural metric on _Y_ ).
