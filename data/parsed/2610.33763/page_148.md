## 9.1 Geometric mean of GLT matrix sequences

We begin by recalling the definition of the geometric mean of two HPD matrices. Given two HPD matrices \(A,B\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}\), their geometric mean is defined by

\(G(A,B):=A^{1/2}\big(A^{-1/2}BA^{-1/2}\big)^{1/2}A^{1/2}=G(B,A).\) (9.1)

This definition preserves symmetry, positive definiteness, and the key variational properties of the two arguments \[3, 16\]. For matrix sequences {_A<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub>_ and {_B<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub>_ of HPD matrices, we write {_G(A<sub>n</sub>,B<sub>n</sub>_)}_<sub>n</sub>_ for the corresponding sequence of geometric means.

The following result, due to \[1, Theorem 4\], extends \[41, Theorem 10.2\] to a broader setting.

**Theorem 9.1 \[1\], Theorem 4)** _Let r_=1 _and_ \(d\ge 1\)_. Suppose_ \(\{A_\mathbf{n}\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\kappa\) _and_ \(\{B_\mathbf{n}\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\xi\)_, where_ \(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n}\in\mathcal{P}_\mathbf{n}\) _for every multi-index **n**, with_ P**<sub>n</sub>** _denoting the set of HPD matrices of multi-index **n**. Assume that at least one of between_ \(\kappa\) _and_ \(\xi\) _is nonzero almost everywhere. Then_

\(\{G(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n})\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\left(\kappa\xi\right)^{1/2},\) (9.2)

_and_

\(\{G(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n})\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_{\sigma,\lambda}\left(\kappa\xi\right)^{1/2}.\) (9.3)

Using the topological a.c.s. notion, we now show that the thesis of the previous theorem holds without assuming that at least one between \(\kappa\) and \(\xi\) is invertible almost everywhere (a.e.), thus proving \[41, Conjecture 10.1\]. We make use of Axioms GLT 1, GLT 2, GLT 3, GLT 4, GLT 5, GLT 6 as reported in Section 4.6.

**Theorem 9.2** _Let r_=1 _and_ \(d\ge 1\)_. Assume_ \(\{A_\mathbf{n}\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\kappa\) _and_ \(\{B_\mathbf{n}\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\xi\)_, where_ \(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n}\in\mathcal{P}_\mathbf{n}\) _for every multi-index **n**. Then_

\(\{G(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n})\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\left(\kappa\xi\right)^{1/2},\)<br>
\(\{G(A_\mathbf{n},B_\mathbf{n})\}_\mathbf{n}\sim_{\sigma,\lambda}\left(\kappa\xi\right)^{1/2}.\)
