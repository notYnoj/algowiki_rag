Applying the well-known binomial series<tbc>

\((1+z)^\alpha=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\genfrac(){pt}{2}{\alpha}{k}z^k,z\in\mathbb{C},|z|\le 1,\alpha>0,\)

<tbc>with \(z=e^{i(\xi_1+\pi)}\), we finally deduce a very informative expression i.e.

\(f_\alpha(\xi_1)=e^{-i\xi_1}\left(1+e^{i(\xi_1+\pi)}\right)^\alpha.\)

In the two-dimensional case, \(\overline{G_{\alpha_1,N_1}}\) has the same symbol as obtained in Proposition 8.2. Similarly, the matrix \(\overline{G_{\alpha_2,N_2}}\) has the same Toeplitz-like structure as \(\overline{G_{\alpha_1,N_1}}\), with parameter \(\alpha_2\) in place of \(\alpha_1\). Therefore, its associated symbol is given by

\(f_{\alpha_2}(\xi_2)=e^{-i\xi_2}\left(1+e^{i(\xi_2+\pi)}\right)^{\alpha_2},\xi_2\in[-\pi,\pi],\)

which is the exact analogue of \(f_\alpha(\xi_1)\) obtained in Proposition 8.2.

**Proposition 8.3** _Let_ \(Q_{\theta,M}=H_{\theta,M}^{-1}H_M\)_. Then,_

\(\{Q_{\theta,M}\}_M\sim_\mathrm{GLT}q_\theta(\xi_3)\) (8.28)

_where_ \(q_\theta(\xi_3)=\frac{1-e^{i\xi_3}}{\theta+(1-\theta)e^{i\xi_3}}\).

**Proof:** In both the one and two-dimensional cases, both _H<sub>M</sub>_ and \(H_{\theta,M}\) are Toeplitz matrices generated by the symbols<tbc>

\(\rho_1(\xi_3)=1-e^{i\xi_3},\rho_2(\xi_3)=\theta+(1-\theta)e^{i\xi_3},\)

<tbc>respectively, where \(\xi_3\in[-\pi,\pi]\). According to the GLT algebra and the standard properties of Toeplitz sequences \[41, Section 6.2\], the matrix sequence \(Q_{\theta,M}=H_{\theta,M}^{-1}H_M\) is associated with the GLT symbol<tbc>

\(q_\theta(\xi_3)=\frac{\rho_1(\xi_3)}{\rho_2(\xi_3)}.\)

<tbc>Therefore,

\(\{Q_{\theta,M}\}_M\sim_\mathrm{GLT}q_\theta(\xi_3).\)
