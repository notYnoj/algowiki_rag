The matrix-valued symbol \(\widetilde{G}(\kappa,\xi)\) is _essentially_ positive semidefinite (see \[3\]) and satisfies the essential support identity

\(\text{essRan}\widetilde{G}(\kappa,\xi)=\text{essRan}\big[(x,\theta)\mapsto\text{ran}\kappa(x,\theta)\cap\text{ran}\xi(x,\theta)\big].\)

Here ran_M_ denotes the column space of an individual matrix _M_, whereas essRan(.) is the essential range of a Grassmannian valued measurable map. The essential formulation of the subspace condition can also be formulated as the pre-image of the essential numerical range in the style of \[\[32\], Definition 1\].

Taking into account the Definition 9.1 of the candidate symbol, we propose the following conjecture:

**Conjecture 9.2.1 (GLT closure under geometric mean)** _Let_ {_A<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub> and_ {_B<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub> be HPD GLT matrix sequences with_<tbc>

\(\{A_n\}_n\sim_\text{GLT}\kappa,\{B_n\}_n\sim_\text{GLT}\xi.\)

<tbc>_Then the geometric mean matrix sequence is again GLT and_

\(\{G(A_n,B_n)\}_n\sim_\text{GLT}\widetilde{G}(\kappa,\xi),\)

_where_ \(\widetilde{G}(\kappa,\xi)\) _is the candidate symbol defined in_ (9.14).

The heuristic justification of the conjecture is the following. For each fixed \(\varepsilon>0\) the shifted sequences \(\{A_n+\varepsilon I\}_n\) and \(\{B_n+\varepsilon I\}_n\) are GLT sequences symbols \(\kappa_\varepsilon\) and \(\xi_\varepsilon\) and their geometric mean sequences satisfy \(\{G(A_n+\varepsilon I,B_n+\varepsilon I)\}_n\sim_\text{GLT}G(\kappa_\varepsilon,\xi_\varepsilon)\). Properties of monotone functions and the matrix geometric mean axioms described in \[3\], combined with closeness of the GLT ∗-algebra (GLT 4) under a.c.s convergence, suggest that letting \(\varepsilon\rightarrow 0\) the limit of \(\{G(A_n+\varepsilon I,B_n+\varepsilon I)\}_n\) is well defined in the a.c.s topology and this limit is {_G(A<sub>n</sub>,B<sub>n</sub>_)}_<sub>n</sub>_, up to zero-distributed perturbations , leading to the GLT algebraic part of Conjecture 9.2.1, a full proof when \(\kappa\) and \(\xi\) do not commute point-wise remains open. Meanwhile, all numerical evidence below supports the conjecture on the spectral distribution part of our hypothesis.
