**Lemma 1.3** _\[85\] For_ \(\xi_i,\varsigma,\gamma,\tau,\upsilon_i>0,i=1,2,\dots,n,n\in\mathbb{N}\)_, we have the following relation_

\(\tau^\varsigma*\tau^{\gamma-1}E_{(\xi_1,\xi_2,\dots,\xi_n),\gamma}(-\upsilon_1\tau^{\xi_1},\dots,-\upsilon_n\tau^{\xi_n})\)<br>
\(=\Gamma(\varsigma+1)\tau^{\gamma+\varsigma} E_{(\xi_1,\xi_2,\dots,\xi_m),\gamma+\varsigma+1}(-\upsilon_1\tau^{\xi_1},\dots,-\upsilon_n\tau^{\xi_n}).\)

**Remark 1.6** _For_ \(\upsilon_1\neq 0,\upsilon_2=\upsilon_3=\dots=\upsilon_n=0,n\in\mathbb{N}\) _the above Lemma reduces the well known result_

\(\tau^\varsigma*\tau^{\gamma-1}E_{\xi_1,\gamma}(-\upsilon\tau^{\xi_1})=\Gamma(\varsigma+1)\tau^{\gamma+\varsigma} E_{\xi_1,\gamma+\varsigma+1}(-\upsilon\tau^{\xi_1}).\)
