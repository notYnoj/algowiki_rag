4. **Structure-preserving preconditioning for variable-coefficient space-fractional diffusion equations.** In Chapter 8, we study the spectral properties and preconditioning of linear systems arising from the finite difference discretization of time-dependent space-FDEs with variable coefficients. The model involves a one-sided Riemann-Liouville fractional derivative multiplied by a variable coefficient, discretized using the shifted Grünwald formula in space and the \(\theta\)-method in time. The resulting all-at-once systems exhibit a (_d_+1)-level Toeplitz-like matrix structure. Using GLT theory, we provide a detailed analysis of the spectral distribution of both the original and preconditioned matrix sequences and propose structure-preserving preconditioners based on circulant approximations. The main novelty is that the analysis fully covers the nonconstant variable coefficient case, both in one and two spatial dimensions, extending several existing results in the literature which were often restricted to more simplified settings.<tbc>

5. **Maximal results for geometric means of GLT sequences.** In Chapter 9, we address a fundamental open question concerning the spectral distribution of geometric means of Hermitian positive definite (HPD) GLT matrix sequences. We prove that when the GLT symbols commute, the invertibility condition on the symbols can be completely removed, resolving a long-standing conjecture. This result is established both in the scalar setting for any dimension and in the general block setting under the assumption that the symbols commute. We further demonstrate that the result is maximal: when the symbols do not commute and are simultaneously degenerate, the classical relation may fail and the geometric mean sequence may not coincide with the classical geometric mean of the symbols. Numerical experiments reveal new spectral phenomena explained by the theory of momentary GLT symbols, including secondary eigenvalue distributions and extremal spectral behavior connected to the order of vanishing of the associated GLT symbol.

Collectively, these contributions provide a comprehensive framework for addressing ISPs in FDEs, combining analytical regularization techniques, spectral analysis of structured matrix sequences, and advanced preconditioning strategies. The thesis advances both the theoretical understanding of ISPs for fractional models and the development of efficient numerical solvers for large-scale structured linear systems arising from their discretization.
