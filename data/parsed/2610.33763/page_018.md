**Chapter 9** is devoted to the analysis of the spectral distribution of geometric means of Hermitian positive definite GLT matrix sequences to the maximal setting, allowing for degenerate symbols and non-commuting cases. It is shown that, when the associated GLT symbols commute, the geometric mean is again a GLT sequence, with symbol given by the geometric mean of the original symbols, without requiring invertibility almost everywhere. This result completes the proof of a long-standing conjecture in both the scalar and block settings, for arbitrary dimensions. The chapter also presents detailed numerical experiments illustrating that, in the presence of non-commuting symbols and simultaneous degeneracy, the classical relation may fail. These experiments reveal new spectral phenomena and suggest a natural candidate symbol for the geometric mean in such generalized situations.

## Main Contributions of the Thesis

The main contributions of this thesis are theoretical, analytical, and computational, and can be summarized as follows.

1. **Inverse source problems for generalized diffusion equations.** In Chapter 5, we formulate and analyze two ISPs for a two-dimensional generalized diffusion equation involving a Caputo-type time-fractional derivative with a general memory kernel and nonlocal boundary conditions. Using the spectral decomposition of the associated elliptic operator, we derive explicit series representations of the solutions in terms of bi-orthogonal systems of eigenfunctions. We establish rigorous existence and uniqueness results for regular solutions and demonstrate that both ISPs are ill-posed in the Hadamard sense. Several special cases, including tempered, multi-term, mono-term, and classical diffusion models, are obtained as particular choices of the memory kernel, illustrating the unifying nature of the generalized formulation.<tbc>

2. **Krylov preconditioning for inverse problems in space-time fractional diffusion equations.** In Chapter 6, we address an ISP for a space-time FDE involving a tempered fractional derivative in the time variable and a Caputo fractional derivative in the space variable. The ill-posed problem is regularized using the quasi-boundary value method. The considered equation is of great interest in applications, and a finite difference approximation of the resulting regularized problem leads to a two-by-two block linear system of large dimensions. A detailed spectral analysis of the associated matrix sequences is conducted using the GLT framework. Based on this analysis, we develop an efficient block triangular preconditioner for the GMRES method and prove that the preconditioned matrix sequence is weakly clustered at one in the GLT, singular value, and eigenvalue sense.<tbc>

3. **Efficient Krylov solvers for inverse source problems in two-dimensional space- time fractional diffusion equations.** In Chapter 7, we extend the previous work to the two-dimensional setting, developing a fast algorithm to reconstruct the source term in a space-time FDE with variable coefficients.
