## General notations and acronyms

• N, R, C: Sets of natural, real, and complex numbers, respectively.<tbc>

• R<sup>+</sup>, N<sup>+</sup>: Sets of positive real and natural numbers.<tbc>

• Z: Set of integers.<tbc>

• K: A field, usually R or C.<tbc>

• K_<sup>r</sup>_: Space of vectors of length _r_ with entries in K.<tbc>

• \(\mathbb{K}^{r\times s}\): Space of \(r\times s\) matrices with entries in K.<tbc>

• \(\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}\): Space of square \(n\times n\) matrices over K.<tbc>

• **_x,y_**: Vectors in K_<sup>r</sup>_ (bold letters usually denote vectors).<tbc>

• _A,B_: Matrices in \(\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}\) unless specified otherwise.<tbc>

• I_<sub>n</sub>_ or _I<sub>n</sub>_: \(n\times n\) identity matrix.<tbc>

• **0**_<sub>n</sub>_ or 0_<sub>n</sub>_: \(n\times n\) zero matrix.<tbc>

• \((\cdot)^\top\): denotes the transpose of a vector or matrix.<tbc>

• \((\cdot)^*\): denotes the conjugate transpose of a vector or matrix.<tbc>

• \((\cdot)^\dagger\): denotes the Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse of a square matrix.<tbc>

• \(\lambda_j(A)\): denotes the _j_-th eigenvalue of \(A\in\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}\), for _j_=1_,...,n_.<tbc>

• \(\sigma_j(A)\): denotes the _j_-th singular value of \(A\in\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}\), for _j_=1_,...,n_.<tbc>

• H: Hilbert space.<tbc>

• \(\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\sigma_z\): Pauli matrices.<tbc>

• \(\sigma^{(i)}\): Pauli operator acting on the _i_-th spin.<tbc>

• _H_: Hamiltonian operator of the system.<tbc>

• \(\rho(H)\): empirical spectral measure of _H_.<tbc>

• \(\chi\): Magnetic susceptibility.<tbc>

• \(\beta\): Inverse temperature parameter.<tbc>

• _Z_: Partition function.<tbc>

• \(\langle\cdot\rangle\): Expectation with respect to the Gibbs measure.<tbc>

• \(A\ge B\), where \(A,B\in\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}\), means that _A-B_ is positive semidefinite. Likewise, \(A\le B\) means that _A-B_ is negative semidefinite.<tbc>
