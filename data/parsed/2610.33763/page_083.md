## 6.2 GLT analysis

This section is devoted to the spectral analysis of the coefficient matrix sequence via the GLT theory, in order to gather information on the asymptotic behavior of the matrices. We start with the sequence associated with the space block _G<sub>n</sub>_, then proceed to the time block _U<sub>m</sub>_, and conclude with the overall coefficient matrix sequence.

**Theorem 6.1** _There exists a continuous_ \(2\pi\)_-periodic function_ \(g_\eta\) _such that_

\(\{B_n\}_n\sim_{\mathrm{GLT},\sigma} g_\eta(\theta),\theta\in[-\pi,\pi].\)

_Moreover,_

\(\{B_n\}_n\sim_\lambda\delta_0.\)

**Proof:** To demonstrate that {_B<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub>_ is a GLT sequence, we claim that _B<sub>n</sub>_ is a Toeplitz matrix generated by a measurable function, i.e., there exists \(g_\eta\) such that \(B_n=T_n(g_\eta)\) for all \(n\in\mathbb{N}\). The idea is simple: we prove that the series

\(g_\eta(\theta):=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\delta_ke^{\mathrm{i}k\theta},\)

where the coefficients \(\delta_k\) are given by (6.8), belongs to the Wiener class, so that \(g_\eta\) is well defined, continuous and \(2\pi\)-periodic, and the corresponding Toeplitz matrix sequence is precisely {_B<sub>n</sub>}<sub>n</sub>_ by definition.

Let us consider the auxiliary function \(\psi_\eta(x):=x^{1-\eta}\). From (6.3), for any index _k_ we have _k_=(_n_+1)_x<sub>k</sub>_. Hence,

\(\delta_k=(k+1)^{1-\eta}-2k^{1-\eta}+(k-1)^{1-\eta}\)<br>
\(=(n+1)^{1-\eta}\big[x_{k+1}^{1-\eta}-2x_k^{1-\eta}+x_{k-1}^{1-\eta}\big]\)<br>
\(=(n+1)^{1-\eta}\big[\psi_\eta(x_{k+1})-2\psi_\eta(x_k)+\psi_\eta(x_{k-1})\big]\)<br>
\(=(n+1)^{1-\eta}\left[\frac{\psi_\eta(x_{k+1})-2\psi_\eta(x_k)+\psi_\eta(x_{k-1})}{\Delta x^2}\right]\Delta x^2\)<br>
\(=\frac{1}{(n+1)^{1+\eta}}\big[\psi''_\eta(x_k)+O(\Delta x^2)\big]\text{as}\Delta x\rightarrow 0,\)

where we used a standard second order central finite difference scheme and the fact that \(\Delta x=\frac{1}{n+1}\). From

\(\psi''_\eta(x)=\eta(\eta-1)\frac{1}{x^{1+\eta}},\)

we have

\(\delta_k=\frac{\eta(\eta-1)}{(n+1)^{1+\eta}}\cdot\frac{1}{x_k^{1+\eta}}+\Delta x^{1+\eta} O(\Delta x^2)\)<br>
\(=\frac{\eta(\eta-1)}{(n+1)^{1+\eta}}\cdot\frac{(n+1)^{1+\eta}}{k^{1+\eta}}+O(\Delta x^{3+\eta})\)<br>
\(=\eta(\eta-1)\frac{1}{k^{1+\eta}}+O(\Delta x^{3+\eta}).\)
