## 9.2.3 Minimal eigenvalues and conditioning

In this section, we analyze the extremal spectral behavior and conditioning of the geometric mean sequence {_G(A<sub>n</sub>,B<sub>n</sub>_)}_<sub>n</sub>_, focusing on its dependence on the analytical properties of the corresponding GLT symbol. This approach follows prior studies on extremal eigenvalues in structured matrix settings, particularly in Toeplitz matrices \[25, 115, 116, 127\] and block Toeplitz matrices \[117, 118\], as well as variable coefficient differential operators, including multilevel cases with _d\>_1 \[98, 139, 126\]. In the case of variable coefficient differential operators it is worth noticing that extremal spectral results do not stem from the GLT theory but from a combination of GLT tools and properties which are typical of linear positive operators \[128, 120\]. Here, we restrict our attention to the unilevel scalar setting with _d=r_=1, considering **Example 1** and **Example 2**.

**Example 1: minimal eigenvalue**

• _X<sub>n</sub>=G(A<sub>n</sub>,B<sub>n</sub>_)<tbc>

• Take \(n_j=40\cdot 2^j\), _j_=0,1,2,3,<tbc>

• Compute \(\tau_j=\lambda_{min}(X_{n_j})\), _j_=0,1,2,3,<tbc>

• Compute \(\alpha_j=log_2(\frac{\tau_j}{\tau_{j+1}})\), _j_=0,1,2.
