In the similar way, we establish the continuity of \(_G\mathcal{D}_t^{(\varrho)}u(x,y,t)\). For the continuity of \(_G\mathcal{D}_t^{(\varrho)}u(x,y,t)\), using Eq. (5.23), we have<tbc>

\(_G\mathcal{D}_t^{(\varrho)}u(x,y,t)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\sum_{k=1}^\infty_G\mathcal{D}_{0+,t}^{(\varrho)}U_{j,k}(t)X_{j,k}(x,y).\)

<tbc>By virtue of Eq. (5.25), we obtain

\(\sum_{j=1}^\infty\sum_{k=1}^\infty_G\mathcal{D}_t^{(\varrho)}U_{j,k}(t)X_{j,k}(x,y)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\sum_{k=1}^\infty\big(-\lambda_{j,k}U_{j,k}(t)+f_{j,k}\big)X_{j,k}(x,y)\)<br>
\(=-\sum_{j=1}^\infty\sum_{k=1}^\infty\lambda_{j,k}U_{j,k}(t)X_{j,k}(x,y)+f(x,y).\)

Using Lemma 5.3, Lemma 5.1 and Eq. (5.43), we can prove the continuity of the series, i.e.,

\(\sum_{j=1}^\infty\sum_{k=1}^\infty\lambda_{j,k}U_{j,k}(t)X_{j,k}(x,y).\)

Furthermore, we have discussed already the continuity of _f(x,y_). Hence, we can say that \(_G\mathcal{D}_t^{(\varrho)}u(x,y,t)\) is a continuous function due to WMT.
