In Chapter 7, we considered an ISP for a time-space FDE with variable coefficients, extending the previous work from the uni-dimensional to the two-dimensional setting. A comprehensive spectral analysis of the coefficient matrix sequences was conducted using GLT theory, which guided the construction of an effective preconditioner. We introduced a block triangular preconditioner and proved that the preconditioned matrix sequence was weakly clustered at one in the GLT, singular value and eigenvalue sense, ensuring fast convergence of the preconditioned GMRES method. Numerical experiments demonstrated the accuracy of the source reconstruction and the efficiency of the proposed preconditioning strategy across various fractional orders and problem sizes. The convergence behavior depends mainly on the spatial discretization, with limited sensitivity to temporal refinement, which is reasonable considering that the number of outliers in the cluster at one of the preconditioned matrix depends only on the spatial dimension. Future work will address extensions to more complex geometries, high-order finite elements or isogeometric analysis with intermediate regularity, and higher dimensions. Additionally, taking inspiration from the variable-coefficient approach in [119] and from the \(\omega\)-circulant approach [31], other options for the choice of the preconditioners should be considered.

In Chapter 8, we studied the spectral properties and preconditioning of linear systems arising from the finite difference discretization of time-dependent space-FDEs with variable coefficients. The model involved a one-sided Riemann-Liouville fractional derivative multiplied by a variable coefficient, discretized using the shifted Grünwald formula in space and the \(\theta\)-method in time. The resulting all-at-once systems exhibited a (_d_+1)-level Toeplitz-like matrix structure. Using GLT theory, we provided a detailed analysis of the spectral distribution of both the original and preconditioned matrix sequences and proposed structure-preserving preconditioners based on circulant approximations. The main novelty was that the analysis fully covered the nonconstant variable coefficient case, both in one and two spatial dimensions, extending several existing results in the literature which were often restricted to more simplified settings. Numerical experiments confirmed the theoretical predictions and demonstrated significant reductions in GMRES iteration counts and computational cost. Several open problems remain. The extension of the eigenvalue distribution results for non-Hermitian Toeplitz matrices to the variable coefficient case seems not trivial and is not covered by the GLT theory due to the intrinsic non- Hermitian nature of the underlying matrix sequences. Additionally, estimates of the number of outliers and of the asymptotic behavior of the outliers in the preconditioned matrix sequences would have precise practical impact in estimating a priori the number of needed iterations of the proposed preconditioned GMRES procedures for reaching a preassigned accuracy.
