## 8 Conclusion

ConStore treats content fetch as a first-class cost of disk- based ANNS retrieval. SICP overlaps bounded content reads with search, while RPAG and GCF reduce fetch I/O through co-retrieval-aware layout. The techniques are complementary and preserve the final top-_K_; together, they reduce end- to-end retrieval latency by up to 48% on DiskANN and 25% on SPANN over the best per-document-read baseline.
