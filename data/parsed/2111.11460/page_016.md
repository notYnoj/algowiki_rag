The protocol proceeds in the following way. Given two binary numbers _b_<sub>2</sub>_b_<sub>1</sub> and _b_<sub>4</sub>_b_<sub>3</sub>, _c_<sub>1</sub> receives both bits _b_<sub>1</sub> and _b_<sub>3</sub> while and _c_<sub>4</sub> receives both bits _b_<sub>2</sub> and _b_<sub>4</sub>. _c_<sub>4</sub> sends (_b_<sub>2</sub> _OR b_<sub>4</sub>) to _c_<sub>2</sub> and (_b_<sub>2</sub> _AND b_<sub>4</sub>) to _c_<sub>3</sub>. _c_<sub>1</sub> sends (_b_<sub>1</sub> _AND b_<sub>3</sub>) to _c_<sub>2</sub> and stores (_b_<sub>1</sub> _OR b_<sub>3</sub>) in _c_<sub>1</sub>. _c_<sub>2</sub> upon receiving the second bit, sends the AND of these bits it received to _c_<sub>3</sub> while storing the OR of these two bits in _c_<sub>2</sub>.
