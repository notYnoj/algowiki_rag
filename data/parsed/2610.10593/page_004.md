## 2.1 Shapes, complete split distributions, and recursive parameters

A level-\(\ell\) shape is a triple _s_=(_i,j,k_) of nonnegative integers with \(i+j+k=2^\ell\). For each coordinate \(w\in\{X,Y,Z\}\), its complete split distribution \(\beta_s^w\) is supported on

\(\mathcal{S}_{\ell,s_w}=\left\{L\in\{0,1,2\}^{2^{\ell-1}}:\sum_tL_t=s_w\right\}.\)

Different occurrences of the same shape may have different parameters; we suppress the occurrence index when it is unambiguous. The root distribution \(\alpha^*\) is supported on all 153 shapes of level 4.

For a positive shape _s_ at level \(\ell\ge 3\), define its allowed half-shapes by

\(D_s=\{u\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^3:u_X+u_Y+u_Z=2^{\ell-1},u_w\le s_w\text{for all}w\}.\)

There are six extraction regions, indexed by permutations \(p_r:\{X,Y,Z\}\rightarrow\{X,Y,Z\}\). At an occurrence _v_ of shape _s_, the parameters comprise a probability vector \((A_{v,r})_{r=1}^6\), a distribution \(\alpha_{v,r}\) on _D<sub>s</sub>_ for each region, and references _c(v,r,u_) to children of shape _u_. The regional complete split distributions are

\(B_{v,r}^w=\sum_{u\in D_s}\alpha_{v,r}(u)\big(\beta_{c(v,r,u)}^w\otimes\beta_{c(v,r,s-u)}^w\big),\beta_v^w=\sum_{r=1}^6A_{v,r}B_{v,r}^w,\) (2)

where the tensor product denotes ordered concatenation of the two split strings. In the family used for the final witness,

\(\alpha_{v,r}(u)=\alpha_{v,r}(s-u).\) (3)

The root distribution \(\alpha^*\) has no corresponding constraint under permutation of its three coordinates.

For a shape with a zero coordinate, one nonzero coordinate’s complete split distribution is free. The other is its image under \(L\mapsto\mathbf{2}-L\), and the zero coordinate is concentrated on the all-zero string. A positive shape at level 2 is a permutation of (1,1,2). It has a parameter \(\mu\in[0,1/2]\): each coordinate of value 1 has probabilities (1/2,1/2) on (01,10), and the coordinate of value 2 has probabilities \((\mu,1-2\mu,\mu)\) on (02,11,20).

The search imposes two further identifications. First, complete split distributions are invariant under the group generated by exchanging the two children of each node in a complete binary tree on the string positions. Only one probability per orbit is stored, with the orbit multiplicity included in normalization and entropy evaluation. Equation (3) makes the recursive product construction compatible with exchanging the two halves. Second, at the transition from level 4 to level 3, children with the same parent occurrence and the same half-shape are shared across the six regions. At the transition from level 3 to level 2, children are indexed separately by parent, region, and half-shape. These choices give 153, 1785, and 41310 stored occurrences at levels 4, 3, and 2, respectively. Unfolding the shared references produces an admissible recursive construction; reuse of a parameter does not identify tensor variables between distinct occurrences.

Including distribution logits, leaf parameters, region logits, and entropy-dual parameters, the search has 212820 real coordinates. This is a coordinate count, not the dimension of the feasible set: softmax gauge freedoms and the paired representation in (3) introduce redundancies. The final witness is expanded into ordinary ordered ternary strings, so its verification does not rely on the orbit representation.
