where N is the sum<tbc>

\(\mathcal{N}=\overbrace{C(t+(l-1)logk)\sum_{i\ge 1}|N_{[B_{i-1}^\prime,B_i)}^+(U_i)|+Ct\sum_{i\ge 1}|N_{[B_i,B)}^+(U_i)|}^{\mathcal{N}_1+\mathcal{N}_2}+\overbrace{C(logk)\sum_{i\ge 1}^{\mathcal{N}_3}|N_{[B_i^\prime,B_i)}^+(U_i)|}^{\mathcal{N}_3}.\)

<tbc>For N<sub>1</sub>+N<sub>2</sub>, because

\(\sum_{i\ge 1}|N_{[B_{i-1}^\prime,B_i)}^+(U_i)|+\sum_{i\ge 1}|N_{[B_i,B)}^+(U_i)|=\sum_{i\ge 1}|N_{[B_{i-1}^\prime,B)}^+(U_i)|\le|N_{[B_0^\prime,B)}^+(U)|,\)

and \(B'_0\ge min_{x\in S}d(x)\), it is bounded by \(C(t+(l-1)logk)|N_{[min_{x\in S}d(x),B)}^+(U)|\). N<sub>3</sub> is bounded by \(C(logk)|N_{[B_0^\prime,B)}^+(U)|\le C(logk)|N_{[min_{x\in S}d(x),B)}^+(U)|\).

Therefore \(\mathcal{N}\le C(t+logk)|N_{[min_{x\in S}d(x),B)}^+(U)|\).
