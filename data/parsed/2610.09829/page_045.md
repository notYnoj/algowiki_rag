For each \(i\in[\tilde{p}]\), as \(\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}_i\) is a solution for \(\Pi_\mathcal{P}(\widetilde{\beta}_i,\widetilde{h}_i)\), it must be the case that cycles in \(\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}_i\) are _d_-diverse (see Definition 5.6). Also, as \(\widehat{\mathcal{C}}\) is a solution for \(\Pi_{\mathcal{P},\alpha,h}(\gamma_i,h_i,\theta_i)_{i\in[b]}\), for distinct \(i,i'\in[b]\backslash\{i_j|i\in[\tilde{p}]\}\), and any \(C\in\mathcal{C}_i,C'\in\mathcal{C}_{i^\prime}\), it must be the case that \(w(C\cap C')\le d\). We will now consider \(i\in[\tilde{p}]\), \(i'\in[b]\backslash\{i_j\}\), and cycles \(C\in\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}_i\) and \(C'\in\mathcal{C}_{i^\prime}\), and our goal will be to argue that \(w(C\cap C')\le d\). Note once we establish the above, it will imply that C is _d_-diverse. For the remainder of the proof we will be relying on Lemma 5.2 without mentioning it every time, in particular we will use that, for any distinct \(j_1,j_2\in[b]\), \(C_1\in\mathcal{C}_{\breve{\beta}_{j_1}}\) and \(C_2\in\mathcal{C}_{\breve{\beta}_{j_2}}\), \(E(C_1\cap C_2)\subseteq E(\breve{\beta}_{j_1}\cap\breve{\beta}_{j_2})\). Due to the above, to prove the required statement it will be enough for us to argue that \(w(\breve{\beta}_i\cap\breve{\beta}_{i^\prime})\le d\). If \(E(\widetilde{\gamma}_i\cap\gamma_{i^\prime})=\emptyset\), then it directly follows that \(w(\widetilde{\beta}_i\cap\beta_{i^\prime})\le d\).
