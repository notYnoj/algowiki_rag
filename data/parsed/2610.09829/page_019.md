Now we are ready to complete the proof of Theorem 1.1 by showing that the instance (_G,V_<sub>1</sub>_,...,V<sub>h</sub>_) of Multicolored Clique is equivalent to the constructed instance \((G',k)\) of Edge-Disjoint Shortest Cycle Packing.

**Lemma 4.4.** _The graph G has a multicolored clique if and only if_ \(G'\) _has a packing of k edge-disjoint shortest cycles._

_Proof._ Suppose that \(\{x_{i_1}^1,\dots,x_{i_h}^h\}\) is a clique of _G_. We construct the packing P of edge-disjoint shortest cycles of \(G'\) as follows. Recall that \(g(G')=L\) by Lemma 4.3. We construct P by using packings of cycles of length _L_ in the vertex and edge selection gadgets. By Claim (v) of Lemma 4.2, for each \(j\in[h]\), there is a packing of two edge-disjoint cycles in _H<sub>j</sub>_ such that the slots of \(S_{i_j}^j\) are free. We include in P these cycles. Consider arbitrary \(j,r\in[h]\) such that _j\<r_. Because \(x_{i_j}^j\) and \(x_{i_r}^r\) are adjacent in _G_, by the construction of \(G'\), there is a pair of two opposite root edges _e<sub>t</sub>_ and \(e'_t\) of _F<sub>j,r</sub>_ such that _e<sub>t</sub>_ is identified with an edge of \(S_{i_j}^j\) and \(e'_t\) is identified with an edge of \(S_{i_r}^r\). By Claim (v) of Lemma 4.1, _F<sub>j,r</sub>_ has a packing of four edge-disjoint shortest cycles that have common edges only with the slot edges of \(S_{i_j}^j\) and \(S_{i_r}^r\), and do not contain other slot edges of _H<sub>j</sub>_ and _H<sub>r</sub>_, then we take these four cycles in P. By doing this for all pairs \(j,r\in[h]\) such that _j\<r_, we obtain P that contains \(2h+4\genfrac(){pt}{2}{h}{2}=k\) edge-disjoint shortest cycles.

For the opposite direction, suppose that \(G'\) has a packing of \(k=2h+4\genfrac(){pt}{2}{h}{2}\) edge-disjoint shortest cycles. By the second claim of Lemma 4.3, each shortest cycle of \(G'\) is a shortest cycle of either a vertex or an edge selection gadget. By Claims (iv) of Lemma 4.1 and Lemma 4.2, each vertex selection gadget contains at most two edge-disjoint shortest cycles, and each edge selection gadget contains at most four edge-disjoint shortest cycles. Since P is of size \(2h+4\genfrac(){pt}{2}{h}{2}\), P contains exactly two cycles from each vertex selection gadget and exactly four cycles from each edge selection gadget. By Claim (v) of Lemma 4.2, for each \(j\in[h]\), there is a unique \(i_j\in[n]\) such that \(S_{i_j}^j\) contains free slots with respect to P. We claim that \(\{x_{i_1}^1,\dots,x_{i_h}^h\}\) is a clique of _G_.

To show the claim, consider arbitrary \(j,r\in[h]\) such that _j\<r_. We prove that \(x_jx_r\in E(G)\). To see this, recall that _F<sub>j,r</sub>_ contains four edge-disjoint shortest cycles from P. By Claim (v) of Lemma 4.1, there is a unique \(t\in[m]\) such that the opposite roots \(e_t,e'_t\) of _F<sub>j,r</sub>_ are in the cycles of the packing P, and all other roots are not included in the cycles. This implies that _e<sub>t</sub>_ and \(e'_t\) are identified with the free slots of _H<sub>j</sub>_ and _H<sub>r</sub>_. Since only \(S_{i_j}^j\) and \(S_{i_r}^r\) contain free slots, we have that _e<sub>t</sub>_ is identified with an edge of \(S_{i_j}^j\), and \(e'_t\) is identified with an edge of \(S_{i_r}^r\). Since _e<sub>t</sub>_ and \(e'_t\) are opposite roots of _F<sub>j,r</sub>_, this means that _G_ contains the edge \(x_{i_j}^jx_{i_r}^r\). Because \(j,r\in[h]\) are arbitrary, we have that the vertices of \(\{x_{i_1}^1,\dots,x_{i_h}^h\}\) are pairwise adjacent, that is, they form a multicolored clique. This concludes the proof.

Taking into account that \(G'\) can be constructed in polynomial time and \(k=2h+4\genfrac(){pt}{2}{h}{2}\), Lemma 4.4 proves Theorem 1.1.
