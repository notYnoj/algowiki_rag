**Handling the general case of Diverse Shortest Cycle Coverage.** The algorithmic steps we discussed for Edge-Disjoint Shortest Cycle Packing up to the greedy choice handling descendants on maximal degree-2 paths of T , remain the same. However, as the strict laminarity property completely breaks down when cycles can share edges, the greedy strategy does not work. More precisely, two cycles in different maximal degree-2 paths may intersect and the greedy strategy falls apart. Instead, our approach is to handle the choices of descendants across different maximal degree-2 paths simultaneously, in one go. To achieve this, we need a better understanding of the structure of the solution we are looking for, as we explain next.
