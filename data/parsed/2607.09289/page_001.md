# Faster Exact Algorithms for Equal-Subset-Sum

Ryosuke Yamano<sup>∗</sup>

Tetsuo Shibuya<sup>†</sup>

**Abstract**

We study exact algorithms for Equal-Subset-Sum in the worst-case setting: given a set _S_ of _n_ integers, find two distinct subsets \(A,B\subseteq S\) whose sums are equal. We establish a new state- of-the-art bound for this problem by improving the fastest known algorithm, due to Randolph and We\(\unknown\)rzycki (S 2026), from _O_<sup>∗</sup>(1.7067_<sup>n</sup>_) time and space to an algorithm that runs in _O_<sup>∗</sup>(1.6994_<sup>n</sup>_) time and uses _O_<sup>∗</sup>(1.5664_<sup>n</sup>_) space. We also improve the best known polynomial- space running time, due to Mucha, Nederlof, Pawlewicz, and W\(\unknown\)rzycki (ESA 2019), from _O_<sup>∗</sup>(2.6817_<sup>n</sup>_) to _O_<sup>∗</sup>(2.5430_<sup>n</sup>_). Finally, we investigate time-space tradeoffs for this problem and improve the running times achievable under a broad range of exponential-space bounds.
