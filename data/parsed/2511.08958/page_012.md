<tbc>A standard dominance implementation yields a transparent match-sensitive bound, while a simple padding identity transfers the SETH-based worst-case barrier from LCIS. The supplied implementation and fresh measurements document both sparse gains and dense overhead. Useful next questions are whether faster LCIS methods preserve all needed endpoint witnesses at their advertised cost, and how much reconstruction space can be saved without recomputing a large fraction of the states.
