**Fisher Ng** is a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington, Seattle, WA. He received the B.S. degree in applied mathematics and the B.S. degree in mechanical engineering from Gonzaga University, Spokane, WA, USA, in 2022, the M.S. degree in applied mathematics from the University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA, in 2025, and is currently pursuing his Ph.D. degree at the University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA. His research interests include time-delay embedding theory, dynamical systems, and multi-scale and reduced-order modeling.
