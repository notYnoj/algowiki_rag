### 5 Conclusion

We have presented an exact algorithm for finding _all_ `type-A` connected maximum common subgraphs, i.e., those maximum subgraphs that preserve connectedness via certain labeling-constraints, of any finite collection of labeled graphs. Our approach relies on combining a pairwise MCS subroutine (realized here via the modular product and a suitably adapted Bron–Kerbosch algorithm) together with several techniques to improve runtime, such as graph-ordering based on similarity measures and techniques for finding `type-A` connected cliques. Indeed, the experiments show that these techniques significantly speed up the runtime of the methods in practice. We have developed a publicly available implementation of these methods (`https://github.com/johannesborg/cmces`), demonstrating its scalability on molecular datasets.
