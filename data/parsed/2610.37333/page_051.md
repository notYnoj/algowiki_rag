## 7.2. Worked Example 1

The movie of Figure 12 was generated by solving Eq. (21) with the regular- solution laws \(\mu_h(c)=\mathrm{ln}[c/(1-c)]+3\left(1-2c\right)\) and _D(c_)=1-_c_ on the unit square at \(64\times 64\) cells, with \(\kappa=0.002\), no flux and no wetting at the walls, and an initial state of uniform composition 0.5 plus seeded noise. Six frames at geometrically spaced times from _t_=0.1 to 1.0 were corrupted with Gaussian noise at 10% of the standard deviation of the noise-free movie and clipped to \[0.01,0.99\] so that the logarithmic term stays finite. The prior scale _s_ was set by empirical Bayes, scanning the Laplace approximation to the evidence at the fit over six values of _s_ and taking the vertex of the quadratic through the three largest, which gave _s_=1.14. Every window was solved with `JIDA` and `cuDSS` in single precision with forward-mode sensitivities, at rtol = atol =10<sup>-4</sup> in the fit and 10<sup>-6</sup> in the sampler, and the fit took 37 s on one NVIDIA A100, compilation included. The posterior was sampled in four replicates of four chains, each for 200 warmup and 100 sampling transitions, with the chains whitened by the Laplace approximation at the fit, at 1.6 to 1.7 h per replicate. All sixteen chains mixed, with a split \(\hat{R}\) below 1.01 and no divergent transition, and the credible bands of Figure 12 are the pointwise 95% intervals of the two laws over the 1,600 draws. The data generation, the fit and the sampler scripts are in the companion repository named in the data availability statement.
