<tbc>of _A<sub>D</sub>_ (where _D_=|F|), and this representation is irreducible for all \(D\ge 4\), it follows that the subspace E_<sub>v</sub>_ generated by orbit differences coincides with the entire space:<tbc>

\(E_v=\mathfrak{d}_0(W_\mathcal{F}).\)

<tbc>We can therefore select _D_-1 permutations \(P_1,\dots,P_{D-1}\in\mathcal{T}(\Gamma)\) such that {_B_<sub>_P_<sub>1</sub></sub>_,...,B_<sub>_P_<sub>_D_-1</sub></sub>} forms a basis for d<sub>0</sub>(_W_<sub>F</sub>). Since \(\mathrm{Tr}_{W_\mathcal{F}}(Z_v)\neq 0\), appending _Z<sub>v</sub>_|<sub>_W_<sub>F</sub></sub> yields a full basis for all diagonal Hermitian operators \(\mathfrak{d}(W_\mathcal{F})\cong\mathbb{R}^D\):

\(\mathrm{span}_\mathbb{R}\left\{Z_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}},B_{P_1},\dots,B_{P_{D-1}}\right\}=\mathfrak{d}(W_\mathcal{F}).\) (D11)

Thus, any diagonal Hamiltonian \(H\in\mathfrak{d}(W_\mathcal{F})\) expands uniquely as<tbc>

\(H=c_0Z_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}}+\sum_{k=1}^{D-1}c_k\big(Z_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}}-\widehat{P}_kZ_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}}\widehat{P}_k^\dagger\big),c_0,c_1,\dots,c_{D-1}\in\mathbb{R}.\) (D12)

<tbc>Since all diagonal operators mutually commute, exponentiating Eq. (D12) yields an exact product factorization for an arbitrary diagonal unitary _e<sup>itH</sup>_:

\(e^{itH}=e^{it\big(c_0+\sum_{k=1}^{D-1}c_k\big)Z_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}}}\cdot\prod_{k=1}^{D-1}\widehat{P}_ke^{-itc_kZ_v|_{W_\mathcal{F}}}\widehat{P}_k^\dagger,t\in\mathbb{R}.\) (D13)

Although a native toggle word realizes \(\widehat{P}_k\) only up to an overall phase, this phase factor cancels out identically under conjugation, ensuring that the phase ambiguity of the toggle implementation does not affect the exactness of the diagonal factorization in (D13).

By (103), each unitary permutation operator \(\widehat{P}_k\) is implementable (up to a global phase) to depth at most \(\Delta_\Gamma\) using native mixer pulses. Each conjugated factor \(\widehat{P}_ke^{-itc_kZ_v}\widehat{P}_k^\dagger\) therefore requires at most \(2\Delta_\Gamma+1\) native operations (1 diagonal pulse drive under _Z<sub>v</sub>_ surrounded by 2 toggle sub-circuits of depth \(\Delta_\Gamma\)). Combining these with the single un-conjugated _Z<sub>v</sub>_ pulse gives a bound on the exact diagonal synthesis complexity.

**Proposition D.1.** Let \(\Gamma\) be a connected graph with \(D=|\mathcal{F}|\ge 4\). Every diagonal unitary operator \(U_\mathrm{diag}\in\mathrm{U}(W_\mathcal{F})\) (including its global phase) can be synthesized exactly using at most

\(K_\mathrm{diag}\le 1+(D-1)(2\Delta_\Gamma+1)\) (D14)

native pulse operations.
