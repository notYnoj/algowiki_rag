<tbc>The _free_ reduced dynamical Lie algebra is generated by taking the Lie closure of the individual local interaction terms in (60):

\(\mathfrak{g}_{\Gamma,\mathrm{free}}^v=\left\langle\{iX_w|w\neq v\},\{iZ_wZ_{w^\prime}|(w,w')\in E,w,w'\neq v\},\{iZ_w|(v,w)\in E\}\right\rangle_\mathrm{Lie}.\) (61)

In contrast, the _standard_ reduced DLA is generated directly by the full reduced Hamiltonians:

\(\mathfrak{g}_{\Gamma,\mathrm{std}}^v=\left\langle iH_M^v,iH_P^v\right\rangle_\mathrm{Lie}.\) (62)

In the symmetry-reduced framework, Theorem VII.3 yields the following analogue for complete graphs.

**Theorem VII.5.** Let \(\Gamma=K_n\) be the complete graph on _n_ vertices, and let \(v\in V\) be an arbitrary reference vertex. The reduced free dynamical Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}_{\Gamma,\mathrm{free}}^v\) and its stabilization depth satisfy
