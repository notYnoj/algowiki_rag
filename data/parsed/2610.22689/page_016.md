## B. Circuit Depth Bounds for Infinitesimal Evolutions of States

Let \(|\psi\rangle\in W\) be a unit vector (\(\|\psi\|=1\)). The real dimension \(m_\psi\) of the projective _G_-orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\) satisfies<tbc>

\(m_\psi:=\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}\big(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\big)=\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}\big(\mathfrak{g}\big)-\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}\big(\mathrm{stab}_\mathfrak{g}([\psi])\big),\) (44)

<tbc>where

\(\mathrm{stab}_\mathfrak{g}([\psi]):=\big\{A\in\mathfrak{g}|A|\psi\rangle\in i\mathbb{R}|\psi\rangle\big\}\) (45)

is the Lie algebra stabilizer of \([\psi]\) in g.

For the DLA filtration \(\{\mathcal{F}_j^\mathbf{a}\}_{j\ge 1}\) generated by the original controls **a**={_a_<sub>1</sub>_,...,a<sub>k</sub>_} (see (32)), define<tbc>

\(r_j(\psi):=\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}\big\{(I-|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)A|\psi\rangle\big|A\in\mathcal{F}_j^\mathbf{a}\big\},r_0(\psi):=0.\) (46)

<tbc>Thus, \(r_j(\psi)\) counts the number of linearly independent infinitesimal directions in the tangent space of the projective orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\) generated by the _j_-th filtered subspace of g. In particular,<tbc>

\(r_j(\psi)=\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}(\mathcal{F}_j^\mathbf{a})-\mathrm{dim}_\mathbb{R}(\mathcal{F}_j^\mathbf{a}\cap\mathrm{stab}_\mathfrak{g}([\psi])).\) (47)

<tbc>Let

\(\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}:=\mathrm{min}\big\{j\in\mathbb{N}|r_j(\psi)=m_\psi\big\}.\) (48)

be the \(\psi\)_-stabilization depth_ of the generating set **a**, representing the minimal number of filtration steps required to fully span the tangent space of the projective orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\).

**Remark VI.5.** It follows from (47) that for all \(j\ge 1\):<tbc>

\(r_j(\psi)\le\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{F}_j^\mathbf{a}),\) (49)

<tbc>which, in turn, implies the state-independent uniform bounds

\(m_\psi\le\mathrm{dim}(\mathfrak{g})\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}\le\mathcal{D}_\mathbf{a}\) (50)

By accumulating the newly generated tangent directions at each filtration step, analogous to the derivation in (42), we obtain bounds on the total number of exponentials, \(\mathcal{T}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}\), required to prepare any target state within a sufficiently small neighborhood of \([\psi]\) in the orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\):

\(m_\psi\le\mathcal{T}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}\le\sum_{j=1}^{\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}}(2j-1)\big(r_j(\psi)-r_{j-1}(\psi)\big)\le(2\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}-1)m_\psi.\) (51)

The following theorem summarizes the primary results established in this subsection.

**Theorem VI.6.** Let \(\mathfrak{g}\subseteq\mathfrak{u}(W)\) be a dynamical Lie algebra generated by **a**={_a_<sub>1</sub>_,...,a<sub>k</sub>_}, let \(G\subseteq U(W)\) be the corresponding connected analytic Lie group, and let \(|\psi\rangle\in W\) be a reference state. There exists a neighborhood \(\mathcal{V}_{[\psi]}\) of the ray \([\psi]\) in the projective state orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\) such that every state \(|\zeta\rangle\) with \([\zeta]\in\mathcal{V}_{[\psi]}\) admits a finite product-of-exponentials realization

\(|\zeta\rangle=e^{i\phi} U_L(\theta)|\psi\rangle=e^{i\phi} e^{\theta_La_{j_L}}\cdots e^{\theta_1a_{j_1}}|\psi\rangle,\) (52)

where \(\phi\in[0,2\pi]\), \(a_{j_s}\in\{a_1,\dots,a_k\}\) for each \(s\in\{1,\dots,L\}\), and the required depth \(L=\mathcal{T}_{\mathbf{a},\psi}\) satisfies (51). In particular, this establishes an upper bound on the local parameterization depth of order \(\mathcal{O}(\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi} m_\psi)\).
