<tbc>Consequently, every permutation \(g\in\mathcal{T}(\Gamma)\) admits a word decomposition \(g=\tau_{v_k}\cdots\tau_{v_1}\) of length \(k\le\Delta_\Gamma\). On the Hilbert space _W_<sub>F</sub>, the corresponding unitary operator \(\widehat{g}\in\mathrm{U}(W_\mathcal{F})\) is synthesized as

\(\widehat{g}=\widehat{H}_{CX,v_k}\cdots\widehat{H}_{CX,v_1}=e^{-i\frac{k\pi}{2}}\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}\widehat{H}_{CX,v_k-j}},\) (104)

yielding a continuous mixer circuit implementation with native pulse depth not exceeding \(\Delta_\Gamma\).

**Theorem VIII.8.** Let \(\Gamma=(V,E)\) be a connected graph with feasible independent-set subspace _W_<sub>F</sub> of dimension \(D=|\mathcal{F}|\ge 4\), and let \(\Delta_\Gamma\) denote the toggle group diameter defined in (103). Then:

1. Every unitary \(U\in\mathrm{U}(W_\mathcal{F})\) admits an exact native Flip-or-Stay pulse synthesis with total depth \(L_\mathrm{glob}(\Gamma)\) bounded by<tbc>

\(L_\mathrm{glob}(\Gamma)\le\Delta_\Gamma(D-1)(4D^2-3D+2)+2D^3-D^2.\) \(\text{(105)}\)

2. For any pair of normalized quantum states \(|\psi\rangle,|\phi\rangle\in W_\mathcal{F}\), there exists a native continuous pulse circuit connecting \(|\psi\rangle\) to \(|\phi\rangle\) (up to a global phase) with total depth \(L_\mathrm{state}(\Gamma)\) bounded by<tbc>

\(L_\mathrm{state}(\Gamma)\le 12D^2\Delta_\Gamma+6D^2-16D\Delta_\Gamma+4\Delta_\Gamma-4.\) \(\text{(106)}\)

In particular, as functions of the feasible dimension _D_=|F| and toggle diameter \(\Delta_\Gamma\), the global depth complexities satisfy the asymptotic upper bounds:

\(L_\mathrm{glob}(\Gamma)=\mathcal{O}\left(D^3\Delta_\Gamma\right),L_\mathrm{state}(\Gamma)=\mathcal{O}\left(D^2\Delta_\Gamma\right).\) \(\text{(107)}\)

**Remark VIII.9.** It is instructive to compare these global bounds with the local-vicinity depth complexities summarized in  IV. Specifically, while global unitary and state-to-state synthesis scale as \(\mathcal{O}(D^3\Delta_\Gamma)\) and \(\mathcal{O}(D^2\Delta_\Gamma)\), respectively, local synthesis bounds are governed by the DLA stabilization depth and orbit dimensions--such as O(D**<sub>a</sub>**_D_<sup>2</sup>) for local unitaries and \(\mathcal{O}(\mathcal{D}_{\mathbf{a},\psi} m_\psi)\) for local states. This trade-off underscores how global pulse schedules incorporate an explicit topological dependence on the toggle group diameter \(\Delta_\Gamma\).

To see how these theoretical bounds manifest in a concrete setting, we next analyze the complete graph family.

**Example VIII.10.** Let \(\Gamma=K_n\) be the complete graph on \(n\ge 3\) vertices. The feasible independent sets consist of the empty set and all singletons:

\(\mathcal{F}(K_n)=\left\{\varnothing,\{1\},\dots,\{n\}\right\},D:=|\mathcal{F}(K_n)|=n+1.\) \(\text{(108)}\)

Identifying the empty set \(\varnothing\) with the symbol 0 and each singleton {_v_} with the symbol \(v\in\{1,\dots,n\}\), the feasible set maps bijectively to {0,1_,...,n_}.

In the complete graph _K<sub>n</sub>_, for any vertex \(v\in V(K_n)\), every independent set distinct from \(\varnothing\) and {_v_} contains a vertex adjacent to _v_. Therefore, the toggle group element \(\tau_v\) associated with vertex _v_ acts as a simple transposition that swaps the empty set, encoded by 0, with the singleton set, encoded by _v_:

\(\tau_v=(0v)\in\mathrm{Sym}(\mathcal{F}(K_n)),1\le v\le n.\) \(\text{(109)}\)

Consequently, the independent-set toggle group is generated by the following transpositions:

\(\mathcal{T}(K_n)=\left\langle(01),(02),\dots,(0n)\right\rangle\cong S_{n+1}.\) \(\text{(110)}\)

The corresponding toggle Cayley graph

\(\mathrm{Cay}\left(\mathcal{T}(K_n),\{\tau_1,\dots,\tau_n\}\right)\cong\mathrm{ST}_{n+1}\) \(\text{(111)}\)

is the classical star graph ST<sub>_n_+1</sub> on _n_+1 symbols. By a well-known result of Akers and Krishnamurthy \[2, Theorem 7\], the exact diameter of the star graph ST<sub>_n_+1</sub> is

\(\Delta_{K_n}=\left\lfloor\frac{3(n+1-1)}{2}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\frac{3n}{2}\right\rfloor=\mathcal{O}(n).\) \(\text{(112)}\)

Substituting _D=n_+1 and \(\Delta_{K_n}=\mathcal{O}(n)\) into Theorem VIII.8 yields the following explicit polynomial circuit-depth scaling for free MIS QAOA on complete graphs:

\(L_\mathrm{glob}(K_n)=\mathcal{O}(n^4),L_\mathrm{state}(K_n)=\mathcal{O}(n^3).\) \(\text{(113)}\)
