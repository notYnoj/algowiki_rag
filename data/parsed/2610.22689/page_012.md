## VI. CIRCUIT DEPTH BOUNDS FOR NEAR-IDENTITY GROUP ELEMENTS AND LOCAL FLOWS ALONG THE STATE ORBIT MANIFOLD

In this section, we analyze the expressibility of parameterized variational quantum algorithm circuits of the form (5) when synthesizing elements within a sufficiently small open neighborhood \(U_I\subseteq G\) of the identity element _I_ in the dynamical Lie group _G_. Our primary objective is to derive an upper bound on the circuit depth _L_, defined as the minimum number of exponential factors in the DLA generators required to generate any element \(g\in U_I\). We first establish a uniform bound for local element synthesis expressed in terms of the _stabilization depth_ of a natural filtration associated with the generators of a dynamical Lie algebra g (see (6)). We then sharpen this bound for local state evolution along the orbit manifold \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\) of a reference state \(|\psi\rangle\). While points states in an open neighborhood of \([\psi]\) within the orbit \(\mathcal{O}_{[\psi]}\) can be expressed as \(g\cdot[\psi]\) for \(g\in U_I\), elements of the isotropy subgroup

\(\mathrm{Stab}_G([\psi]):=\{g\in G|g\cdot|\psi\rangle=e^{i\phi}|\psi\rangle\}\)

leave the projective state invariant up to a global phase. By factoring out these redundant stabilizer directions, we obtain a tighter, state-dependent bound on the circuit depth required to generate local flows along the state orbit. We note that the bounds derived in this section are structural existence guarantees for short-depth local control; they do not explicitly yield the optimal parameter values needed to synthesize a target gate or state.
