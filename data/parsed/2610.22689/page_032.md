<tbc>By the standard structure theory of compact Lie algebras, k decomposes as<tbc>

\(\mathfrak{k}=\mathfrak{z}\oplus\mathfrak{k}_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathfrak{k}_r,\)

<tbc>where z=_Z_(k) is the center of k and each \(\mathfrak{k}_\ell\) is a compact simple ideal. Since \(\mathfrak{g}\unknown\mathfrak{k}\) is an ideal, it respects this decomposition, meaning there exist a central subspace \(\mathfrak{c}\subseteq\mathfrak{z}\) and an index subset \(S\subseteq\{1,\dots,r\}\) such that<tbc>

\(\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{c}\oplus\oplus_{\ell\in S}\mathfrak{k}_\ell.\) (A1)

<tbc>The analytic subgroup _G_<sub>ss</sub> corresponding to the semisimple ideal

\(\mathfrak{g}_\mathrm{ss}:=\oplus_{\ell\in S}\mathfrak{k}_\ell\)

is closed and compact (see, e.g., \[18, Proposition 7.9\]). Consequently, it suffices to show that the analytic subgroup associated with the central subspace c is also closed.

Consider the canonical projection onto the abelianization,<tbc>

\(\pi:K\longrightarrow K/[K,K].\)

<tbc>Since _K_ is compact and connected, _K/_\[_K,K_\] is a compact torus. At the Lie algebra level, bracket terms vanish under the differential \(d\pi\). As {_iH_<sub>1</sub>_,...,iH<sub>k</sub>_} generate g, we have<tbc>

\(d\pi(\mathfrak{g})=\mathrm{span}_\mathbb{R}\big\{d\pi(iH_1),\dots,d\pi(iH_k)\big\}.\) (A2)

<tbc>Let<tbc>

\(\Lambda:=\mathrm{ker}\big(\mathrm{exp}:\mathrm{Lie}\big(K/[K,K]\big)\longrightarrow K/[K,K]\big)\)

<tbc>be the integral lattice of this torus. From the periodicity condition in (10), we obtain

\(\mathrm{exp}\big(\tau_jd\pi(iH_j)\big)=\pi\big(e^{i\tau_jH_j}\big)=I,\)

which implies \(\tau_jd\pi(iH_j)\in\Lambda\) for each _j_. Thus, \(d\pi(\mathfrak{g})\) is spanned over R by vectors in the lattice \(\Lambda\), making it a rational subspace of the toral Lie algebra Lie(_K/_\[_K,K_\]). It consequently integrates to a closed subtorus of _K/_\[_K,K_\].

Equivalently, the central subalgebra c in (A1) integrates to a closed torus \(T_\mathfrak{c}\subseteq K\). Denoting by _G_<sub>ss</sub> the closed compact subgroup associated with g<sub>ss</sub>, the product<tbc>

\(G_\mathrm{ss}T_\mathfrak{c}\)

<tbc>is a compact, connected subgroup of _K_ with Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}_\mathrm{ss}\oplus\mathfrak{c}=\mathfrak{g}\). By uniqueness of the connected analytic subgroup associated with a given Lie subalgebra, it follows that

\(G=G_\mathrm{ss}T_\mathfrak{c}.\)

Being the product of two compact subgroups, _G_ is compact and therefore closed in U(_W_).

_Proof of Theorem IV.4._ Under the first additional assumption, \(\pi(G)\) is a closed subgroup of the compact group PU(_W_), and hence its orbit \(G\cdot[s]=\pi(G)\cdot[s]\) is closed:

\(\overline{G\cdot[s]}=G\cdot[s].\)

Combined with (12), this implies \([t]\in G\cdot[s]\).
