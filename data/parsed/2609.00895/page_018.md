_Proof._ Define

\(R_E:=\Phi^+-\Phi^-+(g_q^+-g_q^-)\Pi_E,R_O:=-\Phi^+-\Phi^--(g_q^++g_q^-)\Pi_O.\) (40)

where \(\Pi_E\) and \(\Pi_O\) are given by (37). Since<tbc>

\(\Phi_q^+=-g_q^+,\Phi_q^--=-g_q^-,\)

<tbc>and \(\Pi_E,\Pi_O\) are monic polynomials of degree _q_, we have<tbc>

\(\mathrm{deg}R_E,\mathrm{deg}R_O\le q-1.\)

<tbc>Using (38), we obtain

\(\Phi^++g_q^+U^++g_q^-V^+=\frac{1}{2}(R_E-R_O),\Phi^--+g_q^+U^--+g_q^-V^--=-\frac{1}{2}(R_E+R_O).\) (41)

Thus it suffices to estimate the coefficients of _R<sub>E</sub>_ and _R<sub>O</sub>_.

By (34) and (27), we may write<tbc>

\(\kappa_{n_s}^{(+)}=(-1)^{n_s}K_0+\delta_s^+,-\kappa_{n_s}^{(-)}=-K_0+\delta_s^-,\)

<tbc>where<tbc>

\(\delta_s^\pm=O(N^{-1})\)

<tbc>uniformly in _s_. Substituting these expressions into (32), and using \(\Pi_E(x_{n_s})=0\) for \(s\in E\) and \(\Pi_O(x_{n_s})=0\) for \(s\in O\), gives

\(K_0R_E(x_{n_s})=\varepsilon_s-\delta_s^+\Phi^+(x_{n_s})-\delta_s^-\Phi^-(x_{n_s}),s\in E,K_0R_O(x_{n_s})=\varepsilon_s-\delta_s^+\Phi^+(x_{n_s})-\delta_s^-\Phi^-(x_{n_s}),s\in O.\) (42)

Set

\(m:=\mathrm{max}\left\{\mathrm{max}_{s\in E}|R_E(x_{n_s})|,\mathrm{max}_{s\in O}|R_O(x_{n_s})|\right\}.\) (43)

By (5),

\(Nx_{n_s}=\frac{N}{\lambda_{n_s}}\longrightarrow\frac{2}{\pi c_s}.\)

Hence there exist constants _C,d\>_0 such that, for all sufficiently large _N_,

\(|x_{n_s}|\le\frac{C}{N},|x_{n_s}-x_{n_t}|\ge\frac{d}{N}\) (44)

whenever \(s\neq t\) belong to the same parity class.

Let \(\ell_t^E\), \(t\in E\), be the Lagrange basis polynomials associated with the _E_-nodes. Writing<tbc>

\(\ell_t^E(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\ell_{t,k}^Ex^k,\)

<tbc>the node estimates (44) imply

\(|\ell_t^E(y)|\le C_1,|y|\le\frac{C}{N},\ell_{t,k}^E=O(N^k),k=0,\dots,q-1.\) (45)

The same estimates hold for the Lagrange basis polynomials \(\ell_t^O\), \(t\in O\).
