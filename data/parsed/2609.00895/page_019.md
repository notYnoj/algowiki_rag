Since \(degR_E,degR_O\le q-1\), Lagrange interpolation gives<tbc>

\(R_E(x)=\sum_{t\in E}R_E(x_{n_t})\ell_t^E(x),R_O(x)=\sum_{t\in O}R_O(x_{n_t})\ell_t^O(x).\)

<tbc>By (43) and (45),<tbc>

\(|R_E(y)|+|R_O(y)|\le C_2m,|y|\le\frac{C}{N}.\)

<tbc>If<tbc>

<tbc>then (45) also gives

\(R_{E,k},R_{O,k}=O(mN^k),k=0,\dots,q-1.\) (46)

For every selected node _y=x<sub>n<sub>s</sub></sub>_, the definitions of _R<sub>E</sub>_ and _R<sub>O</sub>_ give

\(\Phi^-(y)=-\frac{1}{2}\big[R_E(y)+R_O(y)-(g_q^+-g_q^-)\Pi_E(y)+(g_q^++g_q^-)\Pi_O(y)\big].\)

By (44) and (37),<tbc>

\(|\Pi_E(y)|,|\Pi_O(y)|=O(N^{-q}),\)

<tbc>and therefore

\(|\Phi^\pm(y)|\le C_3\big(N^{-q}+m\big),y=x_{n_s}.\) (47)

Combining (42) with (47) and using \(\delta_s^\pm=O(N^{-1})\), we obtain<tbc>

\(K_0m\le\varepsilon+C_4N^{-1}\big(N^{-q}+m\big).\)

<tbc>Hence, for all sufficiently large _N_,

\(m\le C_5\big(\varepsilon+N^{-q-1}\big).\) (48)

Combining (48) with (46), we obtain

\(R_{E,k},R_{O,k}=O\big(N^k\varepsilon+N^{k-q-1}\big),k=0,\dots,q-1.\) (49)
