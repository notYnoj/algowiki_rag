The factor 1/2 accounts for the density of the even integers. With the change of variables _u_=1_/x_,<tbc>

\(\int_{|u|>1}u^{-2q-2}\prod_{s\in E}\left(1-\frac{u}{c_s}\right)^2du=\int_{-1}^1\prod_{s\in E}\left(x-\frac{1}{c_s}\right)^2dx=J_E.\)

<tbc>Similarly,<tbc>

\(N^{2q+1}\sum_{|n|>N\text{odd}}|\widetilde{F}_n|^2\longrightarrow K_0^2(g_q^++g_q^-)^2\frac{(2/\pi)^{2q+2}}{2}J_O.\)

<tbc>Using \(K_0^2=1/2\), we conclude that<tbc>

\(N^{2q+1}\|\widetilde{R}_{q,N}(f)\|^2\longrightarrow\frac{2^{2q}}{\pi^{2q+2}}\left[(g_q^+-g_q^-)^2J_E+(g_q^++g_q^-)^2J_O\right].\)

<tbc>Finally, by (17),

\(g_q^+-g_q^-=A+B,g_q^++g_q^-=A-B,\)

so this is precisely (52).

**Remark 4.2.** For comparison, applying the same Riemann-sum argument to the exact-jumps coefficients _F<sub>n</sub>_ gives (18):

\(lim_{N\rightarrow\infty} N^{2q+1}\|R_{q,N}(f)\|^2=\frac{2^{2q+2}}{\pi^{2q+2}(2q+1)}(A^2+B^2).\)

Thus, so long as the parity balance holds, computing the boundary values from the generalized Fourier coefficients does not degrade the rate of convergence, but changes the asymptotic constant.
