## 4. Analysis of the space-time matrix structures

In the current section we present the spectral analysis of the coefficient matrix sequence using the GLT framework to describe the asymptotic behavior of the matrices. We first examine the sequence generated by the spatial block \(\mathcal{D}_N(a)\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\) then analyze the time block _B<sub>M</sub>_ and finally study the complete coefficient matrix sequence.

**Theorem 4.1** (\[31, Theorem 3.5\])**.** _If_ \(a:[0,1]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\) _is continuous almost everywhere, then_

_a_1) \(\{\mathcal{D}_N(a)\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\}_N\sim_\mathrm{GLT}a(x)f_\alpha(\theta)\),<tbc>

_a_2) \(\{\mathcal{D}_N(a)\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\}_N\sim_\sigma a(x)f_\alpha(\theta)\),<tbc>

<tbc>_where_

\(f_\alpha(\theta)=e^{-\mathrm{i}\theta}\left(1-e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}\right)^\alpha,\theta\in[-\pi,\pi].\)

Since \(\mathcal{D}_N(a)\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\) is not Hermitian, its eigenvalue distribution cannot be directly deduced from **GLT1**, even if additional tools are available; see, e.g., \[9\]. In the constant-coefficient case, the eigenvalue dis tribution satisfies \(\{\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\}_n\sim_\lambda f_\alpha(\theta)\), as a consequence of quite sophisticate results on Toeplitz matrix sequences \[9\]; see also Figure 3.1 in \[31\], where the eigenvalues closely match the samples of the symbol.

In the variable-coefficient case, numerical evidence (see Figure 3.2 in \[31\]) shows a strong agreement between the eigenvalues and the samples of the symbol \(a(x)f_\alpha(\theta)\). However, a complete theoretical proof for the variable-coefficient case is not available and remains an open problem.

**Remark 4.2.** _Let_ \(G_{\alpha,N}=\gamma\mathcal{D}_N(a)\overline{G_{\alpha,N}}\)_. The inclusion of the scaling factor_ \(\gamma=\Gamma(2-\beta)\left(\Delta t\right)^\beta/(\Delta x)^\alpha\) _in the analysis is straightforward and extends the scope of Theorem 4.1. Assume that_ \(a:[0,1]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\) _is continuous almost everywhere and that_

\(\mathrm{lim}_{\Delta t,\Delta x\rightarrow 0^+}\gamma(\Delta t,\Delta x)=\mathrm{lim}_{\Delta t,\Delta x\rightarrow 0^+}\frac{\Gamma(2-\beta)\left(\Delta t\right)^\beta}{(\Delta x)^\alpha}=\gamma^*.\)

_Then_

\(\{G_N\}_N\sim_\mathrm{GLT}\gamma^*a(x)f_\alpha(\theta),\) (9)

\(\{G_N\}_N\sim_\sigma\gamma^*a(x)f_\alpha(\theta).\) (10)
