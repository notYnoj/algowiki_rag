Each multipath in a multi-DAG H=(_V,M_) can be generated by starting with a single multiedge at the source, and then choosing inflow many (i.e. as many as the number of incoming edges of the multipath in B(H)) successor multiedges at the internal nodes until we reach the sink nodes in T .
