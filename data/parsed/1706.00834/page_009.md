## Generalized Weight Pushing.

We generalize the _weight pushing algorithm_ of Mohri (2009) to multipaths to reestablish the three canonical properties of Definition 8. Observe that for every multiedge \(m\in M\), \(\widehat{w}_m=w_m\mathrm{exp}(-\eta\ell_m)\). The new weights are \(W^\mathrm{new}(\unknown)=\frac{1}{Z}\widehat{W}(\unknown)\) where \(\widehat{W}(\unknown):=\prod_{m\in M}(\widehat{w}_m)^{\pi_m}\). The generalized weight pushing algorithm takes a set of arbitrary weights on the multiedges \(\widehat{w}_m\) and changed them into Stochastic Product Form.

Note that the new weights \(W^\mathrm{new}(\unknown)=\frac{1}{Z}\widehat{W}(\unknown)\) sum to 1 (i.e. Property (3) holds) since _Z_ normalizes the weights. Our goal is to find new multiedge weights \(w_m^\mathrm{new}\) so that the other two properties hold as well, i.e. \(W^\mathrm{new}(\unknown)=\prod_{m\in M}(w_m^\mathrm{new})^{\pi_m}\) for all multipaths \(\unknown\) and \(\sum_{m\in M_v^{(\mathrm{out})}}w_m^\mathrm{new}=1\) for all nonsinks _v_. For this purpose, we introduce a normalization _Z<sub>v</sub>_ for each vertex \(v\in V\) :

\(Z_v:=\sum_{\unknown\in\mathcal{P}_v}\widehat{W}(\unknown).\) (3)

where P_<sub>v</sub>_ is the set of all multipaths sourced from _v_ and sinking at T . Intuitively, _Z<sub>v</sub>_ is the normalization constant for the subgraph sourced at \(v\in V\) and sinking in T . Thus for a sink node \(v\in\mathcal{T}\) , _Z<sub>v</sub>_=1. Moreover _Z=Z<sub>s</sub>_ is the normalization factor for the multi-DAG H where \(s\in V\) is the source node. The _generalized weight pushing_ finds all _Z<sub>v</sub>_’s recursively starting from the sinks and then it computes the new weights \(w_m^\mathrm{new}\) for the multiedges to be used in the next trial:

1. For sinks \(v\in\mathcal{T}\) , _Z<sub>v</sub>_=1.<tbc>

2. Recursing backwards in the multi-DAG, let \(Z_v=\sum_{m\in M_v^{(\mathrm{out})}}\widehat{w}_m\prod_{u\in U:m=(v,U)}Z_u\) for all non-sinks _v_.<tbc>

3. For each multiedge _m_=(_v,U_), \(w_m^\mathrm{new}:=\widehat{w}_m\big(\prod_{u\in U}Z_u\big)/Z_v\).

Figure 2 illustrates an example of the weight pushing algorithm. For simplicity, we demonstrate this algorithm on a regular DAG, that is, a multi-DAG where |_U_|=1 for all multiedges \((v,U)\in M\). The DAG on the left shows the unnormalized weights \(\widehat{w}_m\) for all multiedges/edges _m_ in the DAG. In the DAG in the middle, we compute all the normalizations _Z<sub>v</sub>_ for all vertices \(v\in V\) using Steps 1 and 2 of the weight pushing algorithm. Finally, in the DAG on the right, we find the new weights \(w_m^\mathrm{new}\) which are in Stochastic Product Form using Step 3 of the weight pushing algorithm. Lemma below proves the correctness and time complexity of this generalized weight pushing algorithm.

**Lemma 9** _The weights_ \(w_m^{new}\) _generated by the generalized weight pushing are in Stochastic Product Form (see Definition 8) and for all multipaths_ \(\unknown\), \(\prod_{m\in M}(w_m^{new})^{\pi_m}=\frac{1}{Z_s}\prod_{m\in M}(\widehat{w}_m)^{\pi_m}\)_. Moreover, the weights_ \(w_m^{new}\) _can be computed in_ O(_c|M_|) _time where c is an upper-bound on the branching factor of each multiedge (i.e. for all_ \(m=(v,U)\in M\), |_U|\<c)._

**Proof.** First, we show that the recursive relation in Step 2 and the initialization in Step 1 hold for _Z<sub>v</sub>_ defined in Equation (3). For a sink node \(v\in\mathcal{T}\) , the normalization constant _Z<sub>v</sub>_ is vacuously 1. Thus Step 1 is justified. To prove the recursive relation in Step 2, consider any non-sink \(v\in V-\mathcal{T}\) . We "peel off" the first multiedge leaving _v_ and then recurse:<tbc>

\(Z_v=\sum_{\unknown\in\mathcal{P}_v}\widehat{W}(\unknown)=\sum_{m\in M_v^{(\mathrm{out})}}\sum_{\unknown\in\mathcal{P}_v\text{starts with}m}\widehat{W}(\unknown).\)
